# note-100 E7 (f123): ideas

**The shape:** the tiny yellow bud (#FFFE7F, 72x62) at the top of the row of round fruit (peach, coral, pink, blue)
along the right edge of the lumpy green eye bush, lower left. It sits on the bush's crown, with the bush's half-lidded
yellow-and-cyan eye below it.

- **A, own-nature morph: blossom.** 0.2 s: the bud splits at its tip. 1-3 s: five lumpy yellow petals pop open one
  after another, and an orange centre. 4 s: a second ring of smaller candy petals flicks out behind.
- **B, character: a chick hatches.** The bud is an egg. 0.2 s: a zigzag crack opens across it. 1 s: the top of the
  shell pops up. 2 s: a fluffy yellow head comes out. 3 s: its beak opens, chirping. 4 s: two stubby wings flap up
  and a chirp crescent flicks out.
- **C, celebration: a sparkler.** The bud is a sparkler's tip. 0.2 s: it fizzes, and a first spark spike pops. 1-3 s:
  a burst of lumpy candy spikes fans out, bigger each second. 4 s: star-shaped sparks fly off at the ends.

**Pick: B, a chick hatching.** It turns the bud into a creature that could live there (rule 1), with a tiny character
doing something lively (rule 2). It is the brief's "chirp" for the highest notes, quick and bright, with a new beat
each second. A repeats the painting's many flowers, and C is close to plain sparkles.
