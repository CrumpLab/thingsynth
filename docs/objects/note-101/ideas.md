# note-101 F7 (f044): ideas

**What it is:** the little salmon-pink foot (#F7A19E, 57x77) at the end of the purple trunk creature's green leg,
right at the painting's bottom edge, between its pink and red feet.

- **A Kitten paw (own nature):** the foot becomes a paw. 0.2 s: a first pink toe bean pops on its sole; 1-3 s: the
  foot swells into a round fluffy paw, three more beans pop on, tufts of fur stick out; 4 s: the paw stretches its toes
  wide (a bigger pose) with the beans spread.
- **B Tap dance (character):** the foot dances. 0.2 s: the toe flicks up; 1-3 s: it taps down with a lumpy yellow
  impact star at the ground, flicks up again, taps again with a bigger one; 4 s: a big candy "tap" burst and two
  motion crescents beside the foot.
- **C Stomp streamers (celebration):** the foot stomps and party streamers shoot out. 0.2 s: the foot presses down
  and a first candy curl pokes out under it; 1-3 s: fat candy streamers curl up and out either side, one at a time;
  4 s: a fountain of three streamers and a few chunky confetti blobs.

**Pick: B, tap dance.** The creature's own limb does something physical and playful (5a rule 2), with the
celebration in the dance itself, and a toe-tap is the quickest, brightest high-note beat. The paw is a morph with
smallish, fiddly beans; the streamers are decoration.
