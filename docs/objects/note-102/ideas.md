# note-102 F#7 (f160): ideas

**What it is:** a tiny serrated green leaf tuft (#478035 at 0.53, about 70 x 75 px) sticking up at the waterfall's
spout, between the pink flower creature's pale snout and the leafy greens behind it.

- **A, own nature (fiddlehead):** 0.2 s a curled green shoot tip pops up out of the tuft; 1-3 s it lengthens and
  unrolls its crozier, leaflets popping out each side; 4 s the frond is open with a lime curl still at its tip.
- **B, character (grasshopper):** 0.2 s two long antennae spring up from the tuft; 1-3 s the tuft swells into a
  grasshopper's green body, a big hind leg folds up, a round eye pops; 4 s it kicks its leg back and chirps, two fat
  chirp arcs.
- **C, celebration (sparkler):** 0.2 s a bright yellow spark pops on its tip; 1-3 s fat candy star-sparks fizz out
  round it, more each second; 4 s a ring of big lumpy stars bursts round the tuft.

**Pick: B, grasshopper.** The tuft becomes a creature that could live in the grass (rule 1) and does something
physical, and its chirp is a literal reading of a high, bright note (rule 4); the antennae spring up at once for a
tap. The fiddlehead is another spiral (snail, nautilus, rim curl) and the sparkler is close to plain sparkles.
