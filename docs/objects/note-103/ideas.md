# note-103 G7 (f087)

**What it is:** the small lavender nail (85x106) on the thumb of the translucent coral hand, bottom centre: an oval
at the top of the khaki-tan thumb, next to the antler finger, with the lime ground and the dark-green bush behind.

- **A, morph (jewel beetle):** 0.2 s a black head with two antennae pops out at the nail's tip / 1-3 s a seam
  splits it, dark spots come up, the two wing cases lift apart and gauzy wings flutter out / 4 s it lifts off the
  thumb, wings spread, hovering just above it.
- **B, character (thumbs-up wiggle):** 0.2 s the thumb's tip bends forward / 1-3 s it wiggles back and forth a
  pose a beat, the nail flashing / 4 s a big thumbs-up with two motion crescents.
- **C, celebration (violet):** 0.2 s the first lavender petal pops off the nail / 1-3 s four more petals open
  round it and two leaves / 4 s a full violet with a yellow eye in the middle.

**Pick: A, jewel beetle.** The shiny oval nail becomes a creature that could live there (5a rule 1, the way the
acorns became eyeballs), and it quickly pops, splits and flies like a high G7 should: small, bright and fast, the
beetle about 120-150 px. The wiggle moves the coral hand's thumb, whose drawbar already grows a coral branch; the
violet is a generic flower.
