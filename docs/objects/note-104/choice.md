# note-104 G#7 (f096): choice

**Offered:** A bluebell (the bud flares into a bell and rings); B firefly (the bud becomes a firefly and glows); C sparkler (the bud lights like a sparkler, candy sparks fizzing out).

**Picked: C, sparkler** (picked centrally; the firefly went to note 75), with the instruction to make it lumpy candy sparks in the painting's style rather than thin lines. A sparkler is a bright, quick celebration for the top of the keyboard, and the bud already sits on its finger like a sparkler's head.

**Built:** the bud's tip flashes into a white-hot lumpy star with a yellow heart (0-0.06). Fat, pointed, outlined candy sparks then burst out round it in rings of three: yellow (0.04-0.24), pink (0.2-0.4), cyan (0.4-0.6) and orange (0.6-0.8), twelve rays in all at 30-degree spacing. Four fat four-point sparks are flung out between the rays (0.5-0.86). At the end a big lumpy yellow starburst blazes behind them all (0.82-0.96), about 210 px across. Ink 9 px. Everything is `over`.
