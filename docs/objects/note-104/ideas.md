# note-104 G#7 (f096): ideas

**The shape:** a tiny lilac-blue oval bud, about 45 x 85 px, on the tip of one of the pale mint fingers of the lumpy, cactus-like mint hand. The hand sits right of the eye bush, near the magenta creature with yellow horns, and a second blue spike tips the finger beside it.

- **A, bluebell (own nature):** 0.2 s the bud tips over and its end flares into a scalloped rim; 1-3 s it opens into a lilac bluebell about 120 px long, a yellow clapper drops into it and swings, and a sound crescent pops out each second; 4 s it rings, with three fat candy crescents.
- **B, firefly (character):** the bud is a bug's glowing tail. 0.2 s its tip lights yellow; 1-3 s two clear wings pop open, then a dark little head with curly antennae, then it lifts a hop off the finger; 4 s its tail glows big in a lumpy yellow halo, with two motion crescents.
- **C, sparkler (celebration):** the finger is a sparkler's stick. 0.2 s the bud flashes white-hot with a first lumpy candy spark; 1-3 s a crackling ball of fat spiky sparks in yellow, pink and cyan grows round it, bigger each second; 4 s the full fizzing starburst, about 200 px across.

**Pick: B, firefly.** The bud changes in its own nature into a small creature that could live on a cactus (rules 1 and 2), and that is the tiniest, brightest, quickest thing in the batch. It is also not a third plant growth, after the flamingo and the rose. A is a close second (the top note as a little bell's ding), and C is plain sparkle, which loses under 5a.
