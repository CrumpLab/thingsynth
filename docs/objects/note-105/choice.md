# note-105 A7 (f270): choice

Offered: **A** fiddlehead (the leaf curls into a coil that unrolls into a frond), **B** cricket (the leaf is a folded wing:
antennae, legs, a chirp), **C** party blower.

Picked centrally: **A, the fiddlehead** (the grasshopper went to note-102). The leaf grows more of itself in its own nature
(rule 1): a coil on a tap, then a fern frond unrolling a little further each second. Drawn as a few bold scalloped
blade lobes rather than many tiny leaflets, so it reads at the note's small size.
