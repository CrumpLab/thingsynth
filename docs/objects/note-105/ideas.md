# note-105 A7 (f270)

**What it is:** a tiny green thorn-leaf (#4AFF4D at 0.51, 55x74, a pointed triangle) on the left side of the pale
mushroom stem, a little below the bright green cap and left of the curl holding the blue drop.

- **A, fiddlehead (own nature):** 0.2 s: its tip curls into a little green coil. 1-3 s: the coil unrolls outward
  into a lumpy frond, two leaflet lobes a second. 4 s: the frond is out full, its tip flicking a last curl.
- **B, cricket (morph + character):** the leaf is a cricket's folded wing. 0.2 s: two long antennae spring out of
  its tip. 1-3 s: a round head with a bright eye, then a big folded hind leg kicks up, then front legs. 4 s: its
  wings lift and it chirps, three quick yellow chirp-crescents.
- **C, party blower (celebration):** 0.2 s: its point curls and turns candy-striped. 1-3 s: it unrolls out
  sideways as a striped party blower, a mouthpiece end at the stem. 4 s: fully out with a toot, a fringe tassel
  and two confetti blobs.

**Pick: B, the cricket.** A leaf that turns out to be a creature living on the stem (rule 1), with legs and
antennae that move (rule 2), and its chirp is the high note read literally (rule 4). The fiddlehead repeats the
stalks' own leaf growth (Upper 16'/5 1/3'); the blower is a prop.
