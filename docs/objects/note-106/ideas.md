# note-106 A#7 (f273)

**What it is:** a tiny green half-moon leaf (#4AFF4D at 0.51, 57x56) on the right side of the pale middle stalk,
just above where the grey tube crosses it, like a little cupped bud.

- **A, flytrap (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s: the leaf hinges open a crack, a pink inside shows. 1-3 s: it opens
  into a Venus flytrap's jaws, chunky pale teeth along both rims, a second trap sprouts below. 4 s: both snap shut
  together with a little snap burst.
- **B, hummingbird (character):** the half-moon leaf is a hummingbird's body. 0.2 s: a long needle beak juts out.
  1-3 s: a head with an eye, then whirring wings fan up and down behind it, a forked tail. 4 s: it hovers off the
  stalk sipping from a candy flower that opened on the tube.
- **C, lily-of-the-valley (celebration / pun on a high tinkly note):** 0.2 s: a curved stem arches out of the
  leaf. 1-3 s: three candy bells pop open along it one a second, each a little bigger. 4 s: they swing and ring,
  a clapper in each and fat chime arcs.

**Pick: A, the flytrap.** The leaf's own nature as a plant that snaps (rule 1), a jaw that moves (rule 2), and a snap
reads even on a tap. Runner-up C, if the central picks want fewer things splitting open (note-72's pod also opens).
