# note-107 B7 (f095)

**What it is:** the tiny lime spike (about 110x37) tipping the round snout of the waving pink creature, lower left (the balloon animal of lower 8'), pointing right into open orange ground, with the little pink horned figure above and to its right.

- **A sprout (own-nature):** 0.2 s its tip flicks up; 1-3 s two leaves pop from it, then a stem; 4 s a small candy star flower opens on top.
- **B hummingbird (character):** 0.2 s its base fattens into a little head (the spike is its bill); 1-3 s a lime body and a tail pop out behind it, then a blur of wings buzzes up; 4 s it hovers with a pink throat flash and motion crescents.
- **C party blower (party pun):** 0.2 s its tip puffs; 1-3 s a candy-striped paper curl unrolls out of it; 4 s it shoots fully out with a tassel on its end: toot.

**Pick: B, the hummingbird.** The spike already is a hummingbird's bill, so the shape morphs into a creature that could visit that snout (rule 1), quick and buzzy for the top of the keyboard (rule 2). The sprout repeats plants; the party blower is a prop.
