# note-108 C8 (f278)

**What it is:** the see-through pink (#FF4A66 at 51%) half-dome upper lid, 71 x 42, of the small sleepy eye on the
bright green mushroom cap at the top centre, its lilac lower half under it and the stalk below.

- **A. Ladybird (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s black spots pop onto the dome / 1-3 s a head with two antennae comes out
  under it and the dome splits down the middle / 4 s the two halves lift and see-through wings flick open.
- **B. Sleep bubble (character action):** 0.2 s a pink bubble pushes out under the lid / 1-3 s it swells bigger and
  shinier with each breath / 4 s it pops in a splat and the eye jolts open.
- **C. Cherry on top (pun):** 0.2 s the lid rounds into a glossy ball / 1-3 s it swells deep red with a shine, a stem
  springs up / 4 s a leaf pops on the stem: the top note is the cherry on top.

**Pick: A, ladybird.** Rules 1 and 5: the dome turns into something living that could sit on a mushroom cap, with a
physical last beat (wings flicking open), and small, bright and quick for the top note, with no eye-waking. The
bubble ends in the eye opening; the cherry repeats the banana split's cherry.
