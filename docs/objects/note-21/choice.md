# note-21 A0: choice

**Offered:** A, ectoplasm: its hem sags into fat ghost-goo drips that pool round its foot. B, peekaboo: it lifts its
sheet and two eyes peek out from the dark underneath. C, marshmallow: it puffs up, toasts golden and ends speared on
a candy stick.

**Pick: B, peekaboo.** The ghost does something playful and physical with its own anatomy, its sheet (rule 2). That
suits the lowest note: big, slow-building, with the reveal as the payoff. It adds no ghosts, arms or waving, so it
stays clear of the lower 2' drawbar's twins and wave. The goo leaned messy, and the marshmallow needed a prop stick.

**Built:** five tugs. In each, the hem rises in a scalloped edge over a dark plum inside (drawn `on` the pale half,
so the magenta foot and the outlines stay in front). The lifted cloth bunches into a fat roll of a deeper pale blue
(`over`), with its left corner curling up highest. Each roll shrinks away as the next tug lands higher: the corner
first (0-0.1), then 0.14-0.27, 0.3-0.44, 0.5-0.64 and 0.78-0.9. Two big white eyes open in the dark (0.4-0.56),
their pupils and glints pop in (0.56-0.71), and a wide white grin spreads under them (0.86-0.98).
