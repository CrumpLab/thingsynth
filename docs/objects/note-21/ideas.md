# note-21 A0 (f232)

**What it is:** the pale blue (#BEDAF0) left half of the tall two-tone ghost under the big cloud, 168 x 603: a
hooded sheet with the ghost's lime-yellow slit eye near its top and a magenta foot showing at its hem; the darker
blue right half, the red-orange and salmon horns, and the pink cloud on its left are round it. (Its lower 2' drawbar
already brings twin ghosts and a waving lime hand, so nothing here adds ghosts, arms or waving.)

- **A. Ectoplasm (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s its hem sags into a first fat pale-mint drip / 1-3 s more lumpy drips
  lengthen one after another down past its foot / 4 s they pool into a big wobbly ghost-goo puddle round its hem.
- **B. Peekaboo (character action):** 0.2 s the left corner of its pale sheet curls up off its hem / 1-3 s the sheet
  lifts higher like a curtain, a dark gap opens under it and two big round eyes look out / 4 s the sheet held high,
  the eyes wide and a grin under them.
- **C. Marshmallow (pun):** 0.2 s its edge puffs out soft / 1-3 s it swells fat and squashy and its rim toasts golden
  then brown, patch by patch / 4 s a toasted marshmallow ghost speared on a candy-striped stick.

**Pick: B, peekaboo.** Rule 2: the ghost doing something playful and physical with its own anatomy (its sheet),
big and slow-building for the lowest note, with the reveal as the 4 s payoff; it keeps clear of the drawbar's twins
and wave. The goo is own-nature but leans messy, the marshmallow needs a prop stick.
