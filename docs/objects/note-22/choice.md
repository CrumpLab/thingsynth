# note-22 A#0 (f142): choice

**Offered:** A, the banner unfurls (the plume pours on as a rippling candy-striped swallowtail ribbon); B, a genie (a
body and wide arms billow out of the plume, and it grins); C, horn of plenty (big candy fruit tumble out of the orange
horn down the plume).

**Picked:** C, horn of plenty. The plume already is the horn's spill, so the shape does what it is (rule 1), and it is
a celebratory party-food display (rule 3). Heavy fruit landing one after another suit the lowest notes. It is the only
horn of plenty on the keyboard.
