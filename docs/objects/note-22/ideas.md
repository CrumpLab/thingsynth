# note-22 A#0 (f142): ideas

**The shape:** a big see-through yellow plume (#FFFB5B at 0.49, 306x463) pouring out of the open mouth of the orange
C-shaped horn at the left edge (blue collar, red points above and below), sweeping down and left in a curve with two
inner ink stripes, and ending in a notched, two-pointed tip over the green bush.

- **A, own-nature morph: the banner unfurls.** The plume is a ribbon blown out of the horn. 0.2 s: the notched tip
  flicks out longer. 1-3 s: the ribbon pours on in big S-waves, down past the bush, and its two stripes fill with candy
  colours one after the other. 4 s: it ripples at full length, and its swallowtail end flares wide.
- **B, character: a genie.** The horn is a lamp and the plume is the smoke coming out of it. 0.2 s: the tip curls up
  like a tail. 1-3 s: a chest and a round head billow up out of the plume's lower curve, then two big arms fold out
  wide. 4 s: its sleepy eyes open, it grins, and a candy topknot pops on its head.
- **C, celebration pun: horn of plenty.** The orange horn is a cornucopia, and the plume is its spill. 0.2 s: a fat
  pink plum pops out of the horn's mouth. 1-3 s: big lumpy candy fruit tumble out one by one down the yellow stream (a
  pear, a bunch of grapes, an apple), each landing further down. 4 s: a big striped melon rolls out at the tip, the
  heaviest last.

**Pick: C, horn of plenty.** The plume already is a horn's spill, so this is the shape doing what it is (rule 1). It
is a celebratory party-food display (rule 3), in the same vein as the popcorn and the banana split. Big, heavy fruit
landing one after another fit the lowest notes. The fruit stays along the plume, so it keeps clear of note-25's pour.
B was the runner-up, but its body would crowd the orange horn and the painting's left edge, and a smoky spirit echoes
the splash bush's ghost. A is own-nature but lacks a payoff.
