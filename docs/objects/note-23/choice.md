# note-23 B0 (f279): choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A shrimp (bands, legs, fan tail, whiskers), B the big yawn (the leaf drops open as a jaw), C
anglerfish (a lure from its cap, a toothy gape).

**Picked: C, anglerfish.** The hood already bends like a fish's head and its cyan leaf already carries a zigzag of
teeth, so the shape turns into a creature that could live there (rule 1) and opens a jaw (rule 2); the lowest B read as
the deep sea is a big drawn pun (rule 4), heavy and slow-building for a low note.

Built: at a tap the lure's stalk pushes out of the lavender cap's tip (drawn behind the cap); it arches over the mushroom
cap and a pale bulb swells on its end (1 s); a crack of a mouth opens under the cyan leaf and the bulb's core lights
yellow in a glow (2 s); the eye opens looking up at the lure, the jaw drops wide with crooked candy teeth and a pink
tongue, and a spiny coral fin rises on its back (3 s); rays blaze round the lure (4 s). The mouth and jaw are drawn
behind the leaf, so the leaf stays as the upper lip; the fin is under the hood.
