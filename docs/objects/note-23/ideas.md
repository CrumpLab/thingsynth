# note-23 B0 (f279): ideas

**What it is.** A big see-through coral-orange hood (f279, 306x425, top left of centre) that rises from the lime band
and the purple segment of the stack below it and bends over to the right like a bent thumb; a lavender pointed cap sits
on its crown and the cyan leaf with the black zigzag (it reads as a row of teeth) hangs off its right side, the green
mushroom cap behind it and the horned mask to its left.

- **A. Shrimp (own-nature morph).** 0.2 s: a first pale band lays across its bend and an antenna tip pokes out of the
  cap / 1-3 s: more bands segment the hood, a row of little legs ripples out under its curve, a fan tail flicks out at
  its foot / 4 s: two long whiskers sweep up and arch over the cap, the whole hood curled like a big candy shrimp.
- **B. The big yawn (character).** 0.2 s: the leaf's zigzag cracks open into a dark slit / 1-3 s: the leaf drops open
  as a lower jaw, the hood leans back, a pink tongue curls out / 4 s: an enormous slow yawn under the cap, the jaw hanging
  wide.
- **C. Anglerfish (pun: the deepest bass is the deep sea).** 0.2 s: a stalk pushes out of the cap's tip / 1 s: it arches
  over to the right and a bulb swells on its end / 2 s: the leaf's zigzag gapes into a toothy jaw / 3 s: the bulb lights
  yellow and a spiny fin lifts on the hood's back / 4 s: the jaw wide with crooked candy teeth, the lure blazing in a
  halo of yellow rings.

**Pick: C, anglerfish.** The hood already bends like a fish's head and the leaf already has teeth, so the shape turns
into a creature that could live there (rule 1) with a jaw that opens (rule 2), and the lowest B read as the deep sea is
a playful literal pun drawn big (rule 4); it is heavy and slow-building, right for a low note, and stays on the open
side over the mushroom cap.
