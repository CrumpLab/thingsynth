# note-24 C1 (f306): choice

Offered: **A** singing mouth (the gap is the cap's mouth, its thorns teeth, a deep "OHHH"), **B** the bear wakes (a tall
cream bear standing between the stalks hugs them, wakes and yawns), **C** whale song (a candy whale breaches up the gap).

Picked **B, the bear wakes**. The gap turns into a creature that could live between the trunks (5a rule 1), with limbs and
a face that act (rule 2), and the huge yawn reads the lowest note on the keyboard, heavy and slow (rule 4). The face is
drawn the painting's way: almond eyes with pink sleepy lids like the cap's own eye, lumpy lopsided ears and head.
