# note-24 C1 (f306)

**What it is:** the tall cream gap of sky (#FFFCCA at 0.68, 148x524) between the dark left stalk and the big pale middle
stalk, under the grey-green cap with the sleepy pink eye; the stalks' green thorn-hooks bite into it from both sides
like teeth, the slate-blue window f283 (note-71) caps its top and the grey tube crosses its foot.

- **A, singing mouth (own-nature morph):** the gap is the cap creature's mouth, its thorns already teeth. 0.2 s: the
  gap's middle darkens into a plum throat. 1-3 s: it gapes wider, pushing the thorn-teeth apart, a fat pink tongue
  rises from its floor, lips thicken round it. 4 s: a wide deep "OHHH", three slow fat sound arcs rolling out low.
- **B, the bear wakes (character):** the gap becomes a tall cream bear standing between the trunks. 0.2 s: two round
  ears pop up at the top under the cap. 1-3 s: a sleepy face and snout, then big paws reach out and hug both
  stalks, its eyes open. 4 s: a huge low yawn, mouth wide, tongue curled (C1's rumble), drawn heavy and slow.
- **C, whale song (pun on the lowest note):** a candy-blue whale breaches straight up the gap. 0.2 s: its nose and
  a spray of foam break up out of the grey tube at the foot. 1-3 s: its long pleated throat and a flipper rise, an
  eye opens. 4 s: the mouth opens singing and the tail flukes flip out below, two deep song arcs.

**Pick: B, the bear.** Under 5a the gap morphs into a creature that could live there (rule 1, like the pink column's
caterpillar but bigger and heavier), using its limbs and face (rule 2), and the giant yawn reads the deepest note
(rule 4); heavy and slow suits C1. Runner-up A (own form, but a face-on-a-gap); C is a visitor rather than the gap.
