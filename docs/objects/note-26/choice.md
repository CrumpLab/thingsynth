# note-26 D1 (f059): choice

**Offered:**
- A, Venus flytrap: the leaf splits along its midrib and its halves swing open into lobes with pink linings and lashes.
- B, something buried wakes: the leaf is an ear, and a lavender head heaves up out of the slab.
- C, birthday candle: the leaf is a flame, and a striped candle rises under it.

**Picked: A, the flytrap.** The leaf's own midrib becomes the trap's hinge, so the shape opens along the line it
already has. It becomes a living plant with a mouth, and the big, slow opening suits a low note. B repeats the hippo
rising out of the same slab, and C is a prop.

**Built:**
- At 0.2 s the leaf turns into two lavender lobes that have just parted. A pink lining and mint lashes show at the
  seam.
- From 0.4 s to about 3.1 s the lobes swing open in three more poses. Each pose is a bigger, wider lobe, swept in
  round the hinge while the last one drops away. A deep pink throat fills the leaf's place behind them.
- From 3.2 s a gold nectar drop swells at the throat, and it shines at 4 s.

The leaf vanishes as the first pose lands. Everything is placed over. At its widest the trap is about 480 px across.
