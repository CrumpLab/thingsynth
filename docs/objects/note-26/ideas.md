# note-26 D1 (f059): ideas

**What it is:** a tall pale leaf or pod (pale cyan at half opacity, so it reads lavender over the pink wash), about
230 x 330 px. It stands upright on the low tier of the bunny slab, left of the slab's orange hole, with a dark midrib
running from its tip two-thirds of the way down like a seam. The hooked loop of the big peach swoop (note-45) is
about 130 px above it, and the pale pink starfish creature is to its left.

- **A, own-nature morph: Venus flytrap.** At 0.2 s the tip splits along the midrib and a dark crack opens. Over 1-3 s
  the two halves swing apart into big lumpy lobes with candy-pink linings and a fringe of fat soft green lashes, one
  side and then the other. At 4 s it gapes wide, about 450 px across, with a glistening nectar drop at its heart.
- **B, character action: something buried wakes.** At 0.2 s the leaf turns out to be an ear and its tip twitches.
  Over 1-3 s a second ear rises beside it and a round lavender head heaves up out of the slab with sleepy eyes. At
  4 s its eyes open and two paws grip the slab's edge.
- **C, celebration: birthday candle.** At 0.2 s a yellow core flickers at the leaf's base, so the leaf is a flame.
  Over 1-3 s a candy-striped candle pushes up under it and the flame swells in yellow, orange and pink layers. At 4 s
  sparks pop round its tip.

**Pick: A, the flytrap.** The leaf's own midrib becomes the trap's hinge, so the shape itself opens along the line
it already has (5a rule 1). The result is a living plant with a mouth, which gives it some character (rule 2). The
big, slow opening suits a low note. B repeats the hippo rising out of the same slab (voice.manuals). C is a prop.
