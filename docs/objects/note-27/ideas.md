# note-27 D#1 (f064)

**What it is:** a long peach band (601x158) lying low across the orange ground below the big-eye creature: a soft
rounded left end, and at its right end an oval ring that an orange hooked arch dips into and curls up out of, like a
curled tail; the pale cyan pill with the cream cap (note-81) lies just under its right half.

- **A, own-nature morph (rising dough):** 0.2 s the left end puffs up a first lump / 1-3 s lumps rise one after
  another along it, its top splits into a cream crumb seam, a golden crust laps over / 4 s a fat plaited loaf, steam
  puffs curling up from the ring.
- **B, character (dachshund):** 0.2 s two stubby front legs pop down under the band / 1-3 s the back legs, then a
  floppy-eared peach head rears up out of the left end with a sleepy eye that opens, a black nose / 4 s its mouth
  opens panting with a pink tongue out and the orange arch (already its curled tail) gets a wag crescent.
- **C, celebration (party blower):** 0.2 s the left end's tip uncurls a first candy stripe / 1-3 s it unrolls
  straight out to the left, striped pink and yellow, a band at a time / 4 s it is fully blown out with a feather
  tassel fluttering at the tip and two toot puffs.

**Pick: B, dachshund.** The long low band already is a sausage dog's body, and the orange arch in its ring reads as
its tail, so the shape becomes an animal using what is painted (5a rules 1-2); legs, head and panting tongue are big,
heavy, friendly arrivals that suit the low D#1, with the legs giving a tap something at once. The loaf is the
runner-up but reads as food clip-art; the blower is a prop.
