# note-28 E1 (f208): ideas

**The shape:** a tall, see-through pale yellow wisp (#F8FF8F, 294x523) rising behind the acorn frog's back, between
its magenta petal (f217) and the coral tongue's upturned tip, flaring wide in the middle and narrowing to a point that
tucks under the orange drum at its top; its foot is a frayed little fork beside the lower acorn, the pyramid's orange
face round it.

- **A. Calla lily (own-nature morph):** its flaring, pointed form is a calla spathe. 0.2 s: the pointed tip curls out
  and back into a rolled cream lip. 1-3 s: the spathe swells wider and rolls open (1), a fat golden spadix rises out of
  its throat (2), two big lumpy green leaves unfurl from its foot over the pyramid face (3). 4 s: a second, smaller
  spathe pops open beside it and pollen puffs burst off the spadix.
- **B. Sea lion's ball (character):** the wisp rears up as a pale yellow sea lion, nose to the sky. 0.2 s: its tip
  lifts and a dark nose blob pops on. 1-3 s: a round eye and whisker pads (1), two flippers swing out from its sides
  (2), a candy beach ball drops onto its nose (3). 4 s: it balances the ball high, flippers clapping, motion crescents.
- **C. Party streamer (celebration):** the wisp is a ribbon of crepe paper. 0.2 s: its tip flicks out into a curl.
  1-3 s: it unrolls up and over the pyramid face in big loops, candy bands running along it, confetti blobs popping
  round it. 4 s: a second and third streamer burst out of its foot.

**Pick: A, calla lily.** Rule 1 at its plainest: the shape already is a spathe, and it opens into the flower it
looks like, big and slow-building for the lowest note of the batch, every new part the size of the painting's shapes
and kept on the open pyramid face beside it. B is fun but its ball has to go over the drum and frog; C is decoration.
