# note-29 F1: f249, the snout creature's straw

**What it is:** a long pale grey-blue straw (#D1DBDF, opaque, ~640x845) that comes out of the orange nostril ring on
the cream snout creature's pink snout (left of centre), arches up and right over the green stalks, then runs down
beside the yellow spike and ends at the top of the thin waterfall, which pours out of its end into the pool; the tiny
cream gap f307 (note-91) sits under its arch.

- **A, slurp (own-nature):** 0.2 s a fat lumpy pale-blue gulp bulges in the straw at the waterfall end / 1-3 s gulp
  after gulp swells higher up the down-leg, over the arch and toward the nostril, each sinking as the next arrives /
  4 s the last gulp reaches the nostril and a big round burp-bubble pops out of it.
- **B, flamingo (character):** 0.2 s at the waterfall end a pink hooked beak tip lifts out of the water / 1-3 s the
  straw's down-leg turns pink band by band into a long neck and a lumpy flamingo head rises on it, a sleepy eye opens
  / 4 s a big pink wing fluffs open off the arch, the flamingo standing tall over the pool it was drinking from.
- **C, party blower (celebration pun):** 0.2 s the straw's end puffs out a candy-striped tip / 1-3 s a fat candy
  paper curl unrolls out of its end in heavy lurches over the bush / 4 s fully unrolled with a feather tassel flicking
  at the tip and two puff crescents.

**Pick: B, flamingo.** Under 5a the straw changes in its own nature into something that could live there (rule 1): it
already arches down into the water like a flamingo neck dipping to drink. It gains a face that wakes (rule 2) and is
big and slow-building for a low note. A is a close second (physical and funny, but pale on pale). C is a prop.
