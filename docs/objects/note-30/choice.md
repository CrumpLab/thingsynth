# note-30 F#1 (f051): choice

**Offered:**
- A, donut: the band puffs into dough, with icing, drips and sprinkles.
- B, hug: stubby arms come out of the pot and hug the bunny.
- C, pot of gold: gold nuggets and coins heap up, and one flips into the air.

**Picked: A, the donut.** The fat ring is already a donut's shape, so it morphs into something that could live there,
and it is party food for the celebration. B puts limbs on a pot with no face and crowds the bunny. C's coins read as
clip-art.

**Built:**
- By 0.2 s the band puffs into lumpy golden dough, pushed out past its outline.
- From 0.3 s to 1.4 s pink icing pours over its top, swept from left to right. It has a wavy hem, and five uneven,
  bulb-ended drips run down from it one after another, finishing by about 2.2 s.
- From 1.8 s to 3.3 s nine candy sprinkles pop on, one by one.
- From 3.1 s a long drip runs off the bottom, a shine lands on the icing, and a drop falls at 4 s.

Everything is placed over, with outlined and fill-only layers so the drips join the icing seamlessly. All of it stays
inside the painting's bottom edge.
