# note-30 F#1 (f051): ideas

**What it is:** the golden-orange front band of the pot the magenta bunny stands in, on the top tier of the bunny
slab at the bottom right. It is a fat curved crescent, about 500 x 270 px. The pot's peach inner rim and the bunny's
violet base are above it, the slab is round it, and the painting's bottom edge is just below it.

- **A, own-nature morph: the pot is a donut.** At 0.2 s the band puffs up lumpy and doughy, its edges rounding out.
  Over 1-3 s pink icing pours over its top edge and runs down in fat drips, and lumpy candy sprinkles pop on it one
  by one. At 4 s a long drip falls off the bottom and a cream shine lands on the icing.
- **B, character action: the pot hugs the bunny.** At 0.2 s a stubby orange hand pokes out of the band's left end.
  Over 1-3 s two chunky orange arms reach up round the bunny's base, one from each side. At 4 s they squeeze it in a
  hug and the band's two ends curl up like a smile.
- **C, pun: pot of gold.** At 0.2 s the band glints gold. Over 1-3 s lumpy gold nuggets and coins heap up out of the
  pot round the bunny's base. At 4 s one big coin flips up into the air with a sparkle. There is no rainbow, since
  the stream already has one.

**Pick: A, the donut.** The fat ring is already a donut's shape, so it morphs into something that could live there
(5a rule 1), and it is party food for the celebration (rule 3). The drips and sprinkles give a new arrival every
second, kept inside and just round the band. B puts limbs on a pot that has no face, and the arms crowd the bunny.
C's coins read as clip-art.
