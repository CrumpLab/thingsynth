# note-31 G1 (f162): choice

Offered: **A** berry hedge (it heaves up taller and fat candy berries ripen on it), **B** crocodile (its bumps lift as
eye knobs, its spire stretches into a snout, the jaws gape), **C** hedge-hog (spines bristle up along its back, a
snout pokes out, candy apples on its spines).

Picked **C, hedge-hog**. It is a big literal pun, and the hedge itself becomes a creature that could live by the pool
(5a rules 1, 2 and 4). It is heavy and slow-building for a low note. The crocodile repeated the hippo's jaw, and the
berries were closer to decoration.
