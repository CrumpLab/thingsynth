# note-31 G1 (f162): ideas

**What it is:** the dark green hedge on the far shore of the pool (#478035 at 0.77), a long low lumpy mound about
430 x 150 px with two small bumps on its top and a tall thumb-like spire at its right end; the white froth splash sits
against its left end, the pool's top edge runs along its foot, and the bat-wedge (note-74) and the pink band start
just past its right end.

- **A, own nature (berry hedge):** 0.2 s its top lumps bulge up as new leafy humps; 1-3 s it heaves up taller, lump
  by lump left to right, and fat candy berries ripen in clusters; 4 s the whole hedge is twice its height and loaded
  with pink, purple and red berries.
- **B, character (crocodile):** 0.2 s its two top bumps lift as eye knobs; 1-3 s the knobs open sleepy eyes, the
  spire end stretches into a long lumpy snout and nostrils pop; 4 s the jaws gape wide with chunky teeth at the
  water's edge.
- **C, pun (hedge-hog):** 0.2 s the first spines bristle up from its top; 1-3 s rows of fat lumpy spines fan up
  along its back, a pointed snout with a pink nose pushes out of its left end, and its sleepy eye opens; 4 s it is a
  big round hedgehog with every spine up and two candy apples speared on its back.

**Pick: C, hedge-hog.** A big playful literal pun (rule 4) that is also the hedge morphing into a creature that could
live by the pool (rule 1), with a friendly character beat (rule 2); heavy and slow-building for a low note. The
crocodile repeats the hippo's jaw; the berries are closer to decoration. Face on the left end, so it stays clear of
note-74 on its right.
