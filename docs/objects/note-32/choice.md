# note-32 G#1: choice

**Offered:** A, lighthouse: candy bands climb it and its nose becomes a lamp with beams. B, little visitor: the door
opens and a lime alien peeks out. C, rocket salad: big arugula leaves unfurl from behind it and rocket flowers pop
on top.

**Pick: C, rocket salad.** It is a playful literal pun drawn big (rocket is also the salad leaf; rule 4), living
rather than mechanical (rule 5), and it keeps clear of the rocket's blast-off on the lower 1 3/5' drawbar. The
lighthouse's rays echo the sunburst, and the visitor is a character stuck on.

**Built:** six deep-lobed arugula leaves, each 380-410 px long with a pale fill-only midrib. They grow from a root
hidden behind the rocket body (placed `around`, so the mint body stays in front): left (0-0.1), right (0.14-0.27),
up-left (0.28-0.42), up-right (0.42-0.56), then two steeper ones (0.56-0.82). Then three cream rocket flowers with
violet veins and a green-gold eye pop open over the leaf tips (0.82-1).
