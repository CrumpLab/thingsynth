# note-32 G#1 (f309)

**What it is:** the mint (#A5FFD1) body of the little rocket on the golden pyramid's apex, 177 x 535, with a
white-and-cyan porthole and a lime arched door, the white cloud lobe and its pink striped fin behind its right side
and the straw creature's head to the right. (Its blast-off belongs to lower-db7, and "no second rocket", so nothing
here launches.)

- **A. Lighthouse (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s a first red band wraps its base / 1-3 s more candy bands climb it, its
  nose lifts into a railed lantern room / 4 s the lamp lights and fat lumpy yellow beams swing out.
- **B. Little visitor (character action):** 0.2 s the arched door cracks open on a dark gap / 1-3 s a round lime alien
  head peeks out, its eyes open, antennae spring up / 4 s it leans out of the door on its belly, mouth open in a
  hello.
- **C. Rocket salad (playful pun):** 0.2 s a first deep-lobed green leaf tip pokes out from behind its side / 1-3 s
  big lumpy arugula leaves unfurl one after another into a bunch round it / 4 s little cream-and-violet rocket
  flowers pop open on top.

**Pick: C, rocket salad.** Rules 4 and 5: a playful literal pun drawn big (rocket is also the salad leaf), living
growth rather than machinery, and it keeps clear of the blast-off. The lighthouse is a building with rays (echoes
the sunburst); the visitor is a character stuck on rather than the shape changing.
