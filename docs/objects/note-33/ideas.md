# note-33 A1 (f177)

**What it is:** the pink snout (513x240) of the lavender, triangle-headed big-eye creature at the right edge: it
runs from a pointed tip on the left to the painting's right edge, under its round orange-magenta eye, with a red
lip below it and its outline running out past the edge as a white nose-bulb.

- **A, own-nature morph (bubblegum):** 0.2 s a small pink bubble pops out of the snout's pointed tip / 1-3 s it
  swells round and big out to the left, lumpy, with a white shine, then a second deeper-pink bubble swells inside it
  / 4 s a huge wobbling double bubble (about 450 px), a stretched gum strand back to the snout.
- **B, character (sneeze):** 0.2 s the snout wrinkles, two crease crescents pop on its top / 1-3 s it swells and
  tilts up, two nostrils flare at the tip, its lip curls / 4 s ACHOO: a big lumpy burst of white and candy puffs
  blasts out to the left.
- **C, playful pun (big smooch):** 0.2 s the snout's tip puckers into round pink lips / 1-3 s the lips push forward
  into a fat pout and a first heart floats off / 4 s three lumpy candy hearts drift up to the left, the last biggest.

**Pick: A, bubblegum.** The pink snout blowing itself into a pink gum bubble is the shape doing a playful thing in
its own colour and nature (5a rule 1), big and slow-building like a low A1, and it grows out into the open space to
the left rather than over the eye (already the disco ball) or the beak (already the chirping cone). The sneeze is
the runner-up but ends in a generic puff burst; the smooch hearts read as symbols.
