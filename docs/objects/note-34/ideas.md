# note-34 A#1 (f138): ideas

**The shape:** a tall, narrow strip of see-through pale yellow (20% opacity) at the painting's left edge. It reads lime at the top and violet at the bottom where the wash shows through. It is boxed in by the coral-red blade lying over its top, the pink one-eyed finger figure on its right, and the orange cap and cyan creature below. The painting's edge is on its left. No face or limbs.

- **A, fiddlehead (own nature):** 0.2 s its top lifts and curls over as a fat green crozier; 1-3 s a big frond unrolls up past the red blade, broad lumpy lime leaflets popping out in pairs from the bottom; 4 s the frond stands fully open, its last tip uncurling, about 600 px tall.
- **B, the neighbour peeks in (character):** the strip is the painting's edge, and something big lives just past it. 0.2 s one fat lilac fingertip curls in round the edge over the strip; 1-3 s three more fingers curl in one by one, then a lumpy brow and a big sleepy eye lean in over the red blade; 4 s the eye opens wide and a cheek bunches up in a grin.
- **C, lava lamp (celebration/playful):** 0.2 s a first candy blob (magenta) swells at the strip's foot; 1-3 s blobs rise slowly up through it, merging, splitting and wobbling, in orange, lime and pink; 4 s a big blob bulges out over its top under the red blade like a groovy party lamp (drawn `on` the strip, the top blob `over`).

**Pick: B, the neighbour peeks in.** Under 5a the friendly character beat wins (rule 2): it is big, heavy and slow-building, which suits a low note, and the hold's payoff is a huge eye snapping open. It reads the strip's own nature as the place where the picture ends, so it is not stuck on. A is the runner-up as the living own-nature growth, but it echoes the stalks' leaves and the curl. C is a prop.
