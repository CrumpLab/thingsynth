# note-35 B1 (f182): ideas

**The shape:** a long, low band of see-through dusky grey (#6C656E at 0.44, reading olive over the green, 600x349)
running from under the purple ledge's frilly hem rightward under the blue fish's striped head-lobe, notched round it,
and tapering to a point where the fish's body meets the lilac cone; the yellow-green land lies below it.

- **A. Kelp bed (own-nature morph):** the band is a dark sea floor under the fish. 0.2 s: the first wavy kelp frond
  tip pokes up from its top edge. 1-3 s: three or four lumpy olive and mint fronds rise and sway up behind the fish,
  one a second, bladders swelling on them. 4 s: the tallest fronds curl over at the top and a pink one unfurls.
- **B. Crocodile (character):** the long low band with a pointed end is a crocodile lurking in the swamp, snout to
  the right. 0.2 s: two eye bumps lift on its back. 1-3 s: the eyes open, slit-pupilled (1), a row of lumpy scutes
  rises along its back (2), its pointed snout end lifts and the jaws part (3). 4 s: the jaws gape wide in a big grin
  of chunky teeth, pink inside, its tail curling up at the left.
- **C. Flowerbed (celebration):** 0.2 s: a first fat candy tulip bud pops up on its top edge. 1-3 s: tulips spring up
  along it left to right, a pair a second, buds swelling. 4 s: they all burst open at once in candy colours.

**Pick: B, crocodile.** The band morphs into an animal that could live there (rule 1) and does something physical and
playful with its face and jaws (rule 2), heavy and slow-building for B1, along the band rather than spread over the
scene. A is plant growth like the stalks' with no character; C is a decoration.
