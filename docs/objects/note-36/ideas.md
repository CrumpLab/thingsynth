# note-36 C2 (f070)

**What it is:** a pale ice-blue band (398x137) along the front of the orange disc's rim, bottom centre, with an
arched notch cut out of its bottom through which a red shape shows like a lit doorway; the coral neck rises above
the disc and brown legs stand under it.

- **A, own-nature morph (igloo):** 0.2 s a first lumpy snow block pops up on top of the band / 1-3 s rows of lumpy
  blocks stack up into a dome round the arch, icicles bud along its edge / 4 s the dome is closed with a snow cap, a
  warm glow in the doorway and a puff from its top.
- **B, character (mouse hole):** 0.2 s two round pink ears poke up out of the arch / 1-3 s a grey-lilac mouse
  head comes up with a pink nose and whiskers, its eyes, then its paws on the sill / 4 s it climbs half out holding
  up a fat yellow wedge of cheese.
- **C, celebration (cuckoo door):** 0.2 s two little shutters swing open on the arch / 1-3 s a candy cuckoo bird
  slides out on a perch, its crest springing up / 4 s its beak opens wide calling with two song arcs.

**Pick: B, mouse hole.** The arched hole in a band at floor level is literally a mouse hole, so a mouse coming out
is a playful literal reading drawn big (5a rule 4) and a friendly character arriving (rule 2), with the ears giving a
tap something at once. The igloo is the runner-up (the band itself changing) but stacked blocks risk looking like
clean geometry; the cuckoo is a mechanical prop.
