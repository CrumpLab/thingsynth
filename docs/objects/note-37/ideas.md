# note-37 C#2 (f166): ideas

**The shape:** a small see-through violet triangle (#C372D7, 240x202) pointing down, hanging from the horizon line
between the blue fish's raised right fin and the lilac cone (the big eye's beak); mauve and olive sky shows through it.

- **A. Grape drip (own-nature morph):** a blob getting blobbier. 0.2 s: its point bulges into a fat drop. 1-3 s: the
  drip stretches down past the fish's body (1), the drop swells heavy and a second bulge starts beside it (2), the
  first drop pinches off and falls (3). 4 s: it lands in a violet splash crown with droplets.
- **B. Hanging bat (character):** the downward triangle is a bat folded up, hanging from the horizon line. 0.2 s: two
  pointed ears pop out at its bottom tip (upside down). 1-3 s: two round eyes and a little fanged grin (1), its left
  wing unfolds over the fin (2), its right wing unfolds against the cone (3). 4 s: wings spread wide and flapping,
  motion crescents either side.
- **C. Sea sharp (pun on C#):** the triangle is a fin. 0.2 s: its point tips up as it flips. 1-3 s: a violet shark
  body slides out under it (1), its tail fin (2), a toothy grin opens (3). 4 s: it leaps with a splash under it.

**Pick: B, hanging bat.** The triangle's own form unfolds into the creature it already looks like (rule 1) and the
character does something physical (rule 2); its wings are big parts the size of the fin beside it. C's pun is fun but
leans on the note name rather than the shape; A is pleasant but small and slow to read at a tap.
