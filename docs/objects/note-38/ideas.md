# note-38 D2: f264, the pale mint pod sliver

**What it is:** a narrow, see-through pale mint sliver (#BFE6C2 at 0.55, ~155x416) wedged between the straw's
down-leg and the leaning yellow spike, pointed at the top and the bottom, with one round bud bulging from its left
side a third of the way down (a short ink tick inside it), so it reads like a pod with one pea showing or a bud on
a stem.

- **A, pea pod (own-nature):** 0.2 s the round bud swells into a fat green pea / 1-3 s the sliver fattens into a
  lumpy pod, its seam splits open down its length and two more peas bulge out one after another below the first /
  4 s the pod gapes wide, three fat peas in a row, the top one with a glint.
- **B, peekaboo (character):** 0.2 s the bud becomes a round head peeking out from behind the spike, a sleepy eye
  opening / 1-3 s two round ears pop up, a stubby paw curls over the spike's edge, the eye wakes / 4 s it leans out
  and waves its paw.
- **C, pinata (celebration pun on the bud hanging on its stem):** 0.2 s the bud swells candy pink / 1-3 s it grows
  into a lumpy candy pinata hanging on its stem, frills of colour arriving band by band / 4 s it bursts and fat
  candies tumble out down the sliver.

**Pick: A, pea pod.** The sliver changes in its own nature (rule 1): its long pointed form is a pod and the round bud
is already a pea. Each pea is a big new shape the size of the painting's (~120-150 px), so it reads one arrival per
second. B adds another waking eye, and C's burst is a display around the shape rather than the shape itself.
