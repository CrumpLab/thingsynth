# note-40 E2: choice

**Offered:** A, carrot: ridges crease across it and a bushy green top fans up from its root. B, newt: its tip becomes
a head, legs pop out, eyes wake and a tongue flicks. C, horn of plenty: its tip flares open and fruit tumbles out.

**Pick: A, carrot** (made centrally: the horn of plenty went to note 22's yellow plume). The horn turns into
something living that it already looks like (rules 1 and 5), and the drawing grows in a plant's own way, frond by
frond. The newt would put a second character on the ghost's head.

**Built:** five green fronds. Each is a lumpy tapered stem with three lumpy leaflets, and each shoots up from the
horn's root through the gap between the horn and the ghost's head. They are placed `around`, with the ghost (f231,
f232, f233) in front, so the bunch grows from behind its head. The first frond comes at 0-0.14 (its leaflets with it,
so a tap shows it), then 0.18-0.36, 0.38-0.56, 0.58-0.76 and 0.76-0.94. Four short, curved pale ridges crease across
the carrot, one before each new frond (`on`, 0.12-0.8). At 4 s the top is full and bushy.
