# note-40 E2 (f234)

**What it is:** the ghost's left horn, 169 x 354, a red-orange (#FF5E1A) crescent sweeping up and out to the left
from its pale head, its tip up over the pale blue cloud and the pink cloud's top lump. (Kept clear of the drawbar's
twins and lime hand.)

- **A. Carrot (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s a first green frond pokes up from its root by the head / 1-3 s pale ridges
  crease across it and more lumpy fronds fan up behind the ghost's head / 4 s a full bushy carrot top.
- **B. Newt (character action):** 0.2 s its tip swells into a little head / 1-3 s stubby legs pop out along it and its
  bulgy eyes wake / 4 s its tongue flicks out, a creature climbing off the ghost's head.
- **C. Horn of plenty (celebration and pun):** 0.2 s its tip swells and opens a dark mouth / 1-3 s wicker bands wrap
  it as grapes, an apple and a pear tumble out one by one / 4 s a candy and a cherry spill last, an overflowing
  cornucopia.

**Pick: C, horn of plenty.** Rules 1, 3 and 4: the horn's own form changes (it flares open), it is a playful literal
pun on "horn" drawn big, and it is the most celebratory of the three. The carrot is the runner-up; the newt adds a
second character to the ghost's head.
