# note-41 F2 (f128): ideas

**The shape:** the tall, solid magenta petal (#FF47C7, 115x316) standing upright on the left of the pink flower
creature at the top of the river bank, with a small hooked curl inside near its foot. A pink bulb, a purple lobe and a
pale petal sit to its right, a dark olive spike rises behind it, and the green slope and the pool are below.

- **A, own-nature morph: lily.** The closed petal opens into a big pink lily. 0.2 s: its tip peels back. 1-3 s: three
  more lumpy petals unfurl behind it, one each second, curling outward. 4 s: candy stamens spring up out of the
  middle.
- **B, character: a flamingo.** The tall pink shape is the body of a flamingo standing by the pool. 0.2 s: its top
  bends forward as a neck starts to rise. 1-3 s: the neck curves up into an S, and a head with a black-tipped beak
  swings round; one long knobbly leg drops below. 4 s: it tucks the other leg up, stands on one leg, and its eye
  opens.
- **C, celebration pun: ice lolly.** The petal is the shape of an ice lolly. 0.2 s: a wooden stick pokes out under it.
  1-3 s: a bite is taken out of its top, candy sprinkles pop on, and drips run down its side. 4 s: a fat drip falls
  from its foot.

**Pick: B, flamingo.** The petal morphs into an animal that could live right there by the pool (rule 1), like the
narwhal and the giraffe. It does something playful and physical, rising up onto one leg (rule 2). The neck and head
are the size of the painting's shapes, and a tall pink creature reads instantly. A adds to the many plants, and C is a
food prop.
