# note-42 F#2: f248, the pale blue spout

**What it is:** a pale blue curved band (#BFEEFF, opaque, ~280x290) coming out of a small orange ring on the underside
of the cream snout creature's orange snout, bending right and down like water pouring from a tap (an inner line runs
along it like a fold), its foot disappearing into the jagged green bush below; the straw f249 runs down to its right.

- **A, gush (own-nature):** 0.2 s the pour thickens at the ring / 1-3 s it swells into a fat pale-blue gush, lumpy
  drops leap off its bend and a splash crown rises out of the bush where it lands / 4 s the splash crown peaks with a
  ring of big drops.
- **B, genie (character):** 0.2 s the pour's tip lifts out of the bush as a curl of blue smoke / 1-3 s it swirls up
  into a genie's tail and a pale blue body, then a round head with a topknot rises out of it, sleepy eyes opening /
  4 s two arms swing up wide, "ta-da", over the bush.
- **C, watering (celebration):** 0.2 s a first fat drop hangs off the pour's tip / 1-3 s candy flowers push up out
  of the bush where it waters, one after another, each bigger / 4 s the biggest blooms open wide.

**Pick: B, genie.** The ring is already a spout and the pale blue pour already curls like smoke from a lamp, so the
pour itself turns into a character (rule 1) who does something friendly and showy (rules 2 and 3), a playful
literal reading drawn big. A is a generic water motif, and C puts flowers around the shape rather than changing it.
Keep the genie rising up and to the right, clear of the snout's eye and of note-91's gap.
