# note-43 G2 (f175): ideas

**The shape:** a tall, narrow see-through slate sliver (#5B7680 at 0.5, reading olive-gold, 162x396) standing up
between the lilac cone's right side and the pale blue tube, its broad foot sitting on the big-eye creature's pink head
and its top narrowing to a point under a little pink hook.

- **A. Bamboo shoot (own-nature morph):** the sliver is a shoot pushing up. 0.2 s: its tip lengthens and a first node
  ring appears. 1-3 s: segment after segment pushes up out of its top (1, 2), lumpy leaves flick out at the joints
  (3). 4 s: a top crown of leaves fans open.
- **B. Meerkat on lookout (character):** the tall slim upright is a meerkat standing sentry on the creature's head.
  0.2 s: two round ears and a dark nose pop at its tip. 1-3 s: its head swells round with dark eye patches (1), two
  little paws fold in front of its chest (2), it stretches up taller on tiptoe (3). 4 s: it shades its eyes with one
  paw, looking out over the cone.
- **C. Birthday candle (celebration):** the sliver becomes a candy-striped candle on the creature's head. 0.2 s: a
  wick pops on its tip. 1-3 s: stripes wind up it (1), a flame lights (2), wax drips roll down (3). 4 s: the flame
  flares and sparks pop round it.

**Pick: B, meerkat.** The tall upright gap becomes an animal whose posture it already has (rule 1), doing something
playful and alert (rule 2), sized like the sliver itself so it stays clear of the batch's bat on the other side of the
cone. A is growth without character; C is a prop on the creature's head.
