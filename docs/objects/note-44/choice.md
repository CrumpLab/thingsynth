# note-44 G#2 (f291): choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A seahorse (snout, fins, ridges, curled tail), B sock puppet (a mouth, button eyes, singing),
C horn of plenty (fruit and candy spilling out of its top).

**Picked: A, seahorse** (made centrally: the horn of plenty went to note 22). The S-curved horn already has a
seahorse's shape, so it becomes a creature that could live there in its own form (rule 1). Kept clear of note-83's
moon where the horn tips meet: the head and snout come out of the horn's upper bend, below the tip, facing into the gap
between the horns.

Built: at a tap a head bulges out of the horn's upper bend and its long snout pushes out to the right, the mouth
flaring at its end (to 1 s); its eye opens and ridges band its belly one after another, top to bottom (1-2.5 s); a
pink pectoral fin fans out by its neck, then a frilly yellow dorsal fin on its back (2.5-3.4 s); its tail curls out of
the horn's root over the mask's forehead (3.2-4 s). The head, snout and fins come from behind the horn (the tip in
front), drawn as outlined and fill-only layers so they join without seams; the ridges are drawn on the horn.
