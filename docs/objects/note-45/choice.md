# note-45 A2 (f060): choice

**Offered:**
- A, an egg hatches: a chick comes out of the pale bean.
- B, hermit crab: eye-stalks, claws and legs come out of the hollow.
- C, bubble wand: the pale film blows into a big bubble.

**Picked: B, the hermit crab.** The curled salmon swoop is already a shell and the pale hollow its mouth. A character
arriving and waving its claws is friendly and physical. A would be one more bird after the songbird, the cockatoo and
the chirping beak. C repeats the stream's bubbles.

**Built:**
- By 0.2 s two eye-stalks with round eyes pop up out of the hollow.
- From 0.24 s to 1.1 s its coral face fills the shell's mouth and a small mouth appears. The face is drawn on the
  hollow, so the hollow's ink stays on top.
- From 1 s to 2.2 s its right claw reaches out, then its left.
- From 2.3 s to 3.3 s three legs hook over the lip, one after another.
- From 3 s to 3.6 s both claws swap to raised, wide-open poses, snapping hello, and motion crescents flick out
  beside them at 4 s.

The claws go up and sideways, clear of the flytrap (note-26) below.
