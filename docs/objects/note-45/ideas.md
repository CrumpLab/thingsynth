# note-45 A2 (f060): ideas

**What it is:** a pale bean-shaped hollow (pale cyan at half opacity, reading pinkish lilac), about 180 x 220 px. It
sits inside the hooked loop at the end of the big salmon swoop, above the bunny slab, with the loop's red-tinted lip
over its top right, like the mouth of a curled shell. The flytrap leaf (note-26) stands about 130 px below it.

- **A, own-nature morph: an egg hatches.** At 0.2 s a zigzag crack runs across the bean. Over 1-3 s the top of the
  shell pops off and a fluffy yellow chick's head rises out, then its wings. At 4 s its beak is open, chirping, with a
  bit of shell on its head.
- **B, character action: hermit crab.** At 0.2 s two eye-stalks pop up out of the hollow. Over 1-3 s one pink claw
  reaches out, then the other, and a couple of legs hook over the lip as it leans out of its shell. At 4 s both claws
  are up and open, snapping hello, eyes wide.
- **C, pun: bubble wand.** At 0.2 s the pale film bulges out of the loop. Over 1-3 s it swells into a big candy-sheen
  bubble and pinches off. At 4 s the bubble floats up and left with two small ones after it.

**Pick: B, the hermit crab.** The curled swoop is already a shell and the pale hollow its mouth, so it is the shape's
own nature (5a rule 1). A character arriving and waving its claws is friendly and physical (rule 2). Its claws go
up and sideways, clear of the flytrap below. A is one more bird beside the songbird, the cockatoo and the chirping
beak. C repeats the stream's bubbles.
