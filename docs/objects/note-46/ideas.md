# note-46 A#2 (f185): ideas

**The shape:** an opaque coral-pink sausage, a curved tongue-like lip (#FF7580, 315x173), lying under the acorn frog's
little blue bell-shaped head, joined to its chin by the thin pink strand and running into the long salmon band; the
frog's pale yellow body is above it, lime ground below.

- **A. Bubblegum (own-nature morph):** the pink lip pouts and blows. 0.2 s: its left end puffs into a small pink
  bubble. 1-3 s: the bubble swells round and shiny (1), bigger, with a highlight (2), huge and wobbly over the frog's
  chin (3). 4 s: it pops into ragged pink tatters with a pop star.
- **B. Frog tongue catches a fly (character):** it is the frog's tongue. 0.2 s: its tip lifts and curls up off the
  ground. 1-3 s: a big candy fly buzzes in at the left (1), the tongue unrolls in a long sticky arc toward it (2), its
  tip curls round the fly (3). 4 s: it snaps the fly up beside the frog's head and the frog's cheek flushes, motion
  crescents round it.
- **C. Party blower (celebration):** 0.2 s: its tip uncurls a little. 1-3 s: it unrolls longer as a striped party
  blower out of the frog's mouth (1, 2), toot puffs come out its end (3). 4 s: a feather tassel pops on its tip.

**Pick: B, frog tongue.** It uses the frog's own anatomy (rule 2: he knows his characters' anatomy) and is the tongue
doing what a frog's tongue does, physical and playful, in its own nature (rule 1). A is good but its pop at the end
reads as the bubble vanishing; C is a prop.
