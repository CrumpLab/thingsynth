# note-47 B2 (f079)

**What it is:** the flat orange lozenge (about 330x95, opaque #FFA648) at the bottom centre that the pink figure's long lime-khaki neck rises out of (lower 4': its cyan ring head with the purple tongue is up and to the right), with the magenta ear flap at its right end, the brown wisp hanging off its left end into the ground's dish (the pedal split's sauce pour) and the long red arm lying under it.

- **A turtle (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s its top edge bulges up into the rim of a dome; 1-3 s it domes up into a big orange shell (about 450 px) and lumpy candy-edged scute plates pop on it one by one while stubby legs push out under it front and back; 4 s a tail flicks out and the front legs lift mid-stride: the long-necked figure turns out to be a turtle's neck.
- **B seal wakes (character):** 0.2 s its right end lifts into a snout; 1-3 s a sleepy eye opens, whiskers bristle, two flippers flop out and clap; 4 s a candy ball pops onto its nose.
- **C pool party float (celebration):** 0.2 s it starts to puff up; 1-3 s it inflates into a fat inner tube round the neck and candy stripes band it one by one; 4 s a ring of splash drops leaps up round it.

**Pick: A, the turtle.** The disc's own role (the thing the long neck comes out of) morphs into the thing that would be there (rule 1), with legs that do something physical (rule 2), and a heavy shell built up slowly suits a low B2. The seal adds a second face to a creature that already has one; the float is a prop.
