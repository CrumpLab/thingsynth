# note-48 C3 (f113): ideas

**The shape:** a narrow, bright magenta stalk about 500 px long, rising from the top of the soft cushion mound (the octopus) up into the green lobes of the splash bush (the ghost). It runs between the bush's lobes on its left and the green eye tree's crown on its right, and the cushion's tentacle curls behind its foot. Only its middle 400 px are open.

- **A, flamingo leg (own nature):** the stalk was a flamingo's leg all along. 0.2 s a knobbly backwards knee bulges halfway up it; 1-3 s a webbed pink foot lifts and tucks up under it so it stands on one leg, then a long pink neck curls out from the bush's right side; 4 s the head comes round with its black-tipped bent beak and a sleepy eye, a little feather tuft on its back.
- **B, koala hug (character):** 0.2 s two round grey ears pop out from behind the stalk; 1-3 s a lumpy grey koala climbs up, its arms wrap round the stalk, then its big black nose and sleepy eyes come round; 4 s it hugs the stalk tight and its cheek squashes against it.
- **C, candy floss (celebration):** the stalk becomes a fairground candy-floss stick. 0.2 s a first pink wisp-puff swells on the stalk's open middle; 1-3 s puffs of pink and lilac floss wind round and pile up, bigger each second; 4 s a huge lumpy cloud of floss with a few candy sprinkles.

**Pick: A, flamingo leg.** It is the stalk's own form doing something: a big drawn, playful literal pun (a magenta stalk is a flamingo's leg), with a character arriving at the end (rules 1, 2 and 4). It also stays in the open strip between the bush and the eye tree. B is a visitor, not the stalk, and C is party food stuck on.
