# note-49 C#3 (f058): choice

**Offered:**
- A, a pea pod pops: the seam splits and fat candy peas pop out.
- B, a gecko: a gecko on the cactus.
- C, the cactus flowers: the almond is a bud that opens into a cactus bloom.

**Picked: C, the cactus bloom.** This was chosen centrally: the pea pod went to note-38. Asked for: waxy and
many-petalled, on the spiny lobe, not a daisy. The almond is a bud at the foot of the bunny's spiny lobe, which reads
as a cactus, so the flower is its own nature.

**Built:**
- By 0.2 s the bud's tip parts and three pink petal tips poke out.
- From 0.24 s to 1.5 s nine broad hot-pink outer petals open, one after another.
- From 1.3 s to 2.5 s eight mid-pink petals open.
- From 2.3 s to 3.4 s six pale pink inner petals open.
- From 3.1 s to 3.5 s a fuzzy yellow stamen mass swells at the heart, then a lime stigma.
- From 3.6 s to 4 s waxy shines land on two petals.

The petals are spoon-shaped and slightly pointed, layered and overlapping, and stagger toward the tip so the flower
reads as a cup tilted toward us. The upper half of the almond stays as its tube against the lobe. Everything is placed
over. The flower is about 330 px across.
