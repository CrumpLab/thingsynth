# note-49 C#3 (f058): ideas

**What it is:** a pale cyan almond, about 220 x 190 px, with a crease along its length like a pod's seam. It lies
diagonally, pointing down-left, at the foot of the magenta bunny's leaning left lobe. The lobe has little spine
ticks, so the bunny reads like a cactus. Peach ground is round it, and the bunny's pot is below and to its right.

- **A, own-nature morph: a pea pod pops.** At 0.2 s the seam cracks open along its length. Over 1-3 s the halves
  spread and three fat peas pop out one after another, each a different candy colour, and bounce down-left. At 4 s
  the empty pod gapes wide and the last and biggest pea is in the air.
- **B, character action: a gecko on the cactus.** At 0.2 s a head pops out of the almond's tip and blinks. Over
  1-3 s four splayed legs grip the lobe and a tail curls out behind. At 4 s it is in full candy spots, with its tongue
  flicking out.
- **C, celebration: the cactus flowers.** At 0.2 s the almond's tip parts, showing that it is a bud. Over 1-3 s
  lumpy candy petals unfurl in layers into a big frilly cactus flower on the bunny's arm. At 4 s yellow stamens pop
  in its middle.

**Pick: A, the pea pod.** The crease the shape already has becomes the seam that splits (5a rule 1). Popping peas
are bright and bouncy for a middle note, and each pop is a new arrival. B is a creature stuck on rather than the
shape changing. C repeats the straw creature's bloom.
