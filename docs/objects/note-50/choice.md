# note-50 D3 (f265): choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A beetle (wing cases lift, wings unfold), B peek-a-boo (a little creature under the lid), C
pinata (fringe, a crack, a burst of candy).

**Picked: C, pinata** (made centrally: the jewel beetle went to note 103). The rounded purple segment becomes a
pinata, the party thing it already looks like, and it does something physical and celebratory (rules 1 and 3).

Built: at a tap a band of pink crepe-paper fringe appears across its top, then a yellow one and a cyan one (to 1.4 s,
drawn on the segment); a crack zigzags across it and widens (1.3-2.5 s); it bursts open into a jagged dark hole packed
with candy and two paper shards fly off (2.2-2.8 s); candies tumble out of the hole one after another, a pink wrapped
sweet, a mint gumdrop, a lollipop, a lilac ball, a cyan sweet, an orange gumdrop, spilling round the lavender cone
(2.5-3.8 s); confetti bursts out last (3.4-4 s). Each candy grows out from the hole's centre, so it flies out.
