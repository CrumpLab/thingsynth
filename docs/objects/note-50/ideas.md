# note-50 D3 (f265): ideas

**What it is.** The rounded purple segment (f265, 130x178) in the little stack under the coral-orange hood: a lime band
sits on it like a lid or collar and the pale lavender cone spreads out below it like a skirt; it is a translucent violet
with the wash showing through, between the horned mask and the yellow-capped pink mound.

- **A. Beetle (own-nature morph).** 0.2 s: a seam splits down its middle and the halves part a crack / 1-3 s: the two
  purple wing cases lift apart, lacy pale wings unfold from between them, three stubby legs a side poke out / 4 s: two
  knobbed antennae curl up from the lime band and motion crescents buzz round the open wings.
- **B. Peek-a-boo (character).** 0.2 s: the lime band lifts like a lid / 1-3 s: two eyes peep over the rim, a little
  purple creature rises and grips the rim / 4 s: it waves.
- **C. Pinata (celebration).** 0.2 s: a crack zigzags across it / 1-3 s: a fringe of paper flaps out, it bursts into
  pieces / 4 s: candies tumble down the lavender cone in a heap.

**Pick: A, beetle.** Its rounded shell becomes a creature that could live there (rule 1) and splits open in its own form
like the coral tongue, physical and lively for a middle note, with parts at about the segment's size. The pinata is the
runner-up (celebratory, physical) but spreads small bits; peek-a-boo is another face and another wave.
