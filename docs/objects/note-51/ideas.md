# note-51 D#3 (f259): ideas

**The shape:** the pale blue band (#D3E8FF at 0.73, 179x134) along the bottom of the round blue bell that the pink
cross skewers. It is the bell's lip or hem, the yellow pour of note-25 falls from under it, and a red curl sticks out
of the bell's right.

- **A, own-nature morph: icicles.** The pale lip freezes. 0.2 s: a first lumpy icicle drips down from its edge. 1-3 s:
  two more icicles grow, each longer, and frost crusts the rim. 4 s: the longest one swells a drop at its tip that
  glints.
- **B, character: a ballerina.** The pink cross is a dancer's arms and body, the blue bell her bodice, and the band
  her hem. 0.2 s: the hem frills out into a ruffle. 1-3 s: it spreads into a layered tutu, and one striped leg kicks
  out to the left, then the other to the right. 4 s: both legs reach full extension in a leap, pink pointe shoes on
  the ends.
- **C, celebration: a birthday cupcake.** The band is a cupcake's wrapper, and the blue dome is its frosting. 0.2 s:
  the band crinkles into pleats. 1-3 s: candy sprinkles pop onto the blue dome a few at a time, and a cherry lands.
  4 s: a flame lights on the pink stick's tip, which turns out to be a candle.

**Pick: B, ballerina.** It reads the anatomy that is already painted (the cross as arms), so a thing with no face
becomes a character doing something physical and joyful (rule 2), and a dance is a celebration (rule 3). The legs kick
out to the sides, not straight down, so they keep off note-25's pour. A is a quieter own-nature drip that hangs over
note-25. C is party decoration spread over shapes that aren't its own.
