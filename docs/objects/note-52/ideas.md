# note-52 E3 (f108)

**What it is:** a pale cream, half see-through pennant (about 195x225, reads pale lime over the wash) hanging point-down from the wavy underside of the green bank, between the little pink horned figure on its left and the pink figure's big lime neck curve on its right, the cyan ring head up and to its right.

- **A cream drip (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s its point swells into a bead; 1-3 s it stretches longer, the bead fattens and necks in; 4 s the drop falls free and lands in a crown splash below.
- **B bat (character):** 0.2 s two pointed ears prick out at its tip (it hangs upside down); 1-3 s a sleepy eye opens, then one wing unfolds out to the left and the other to the right, lumpy and scalloped; 4 s it spreads wide and squeaks, mouth open, with motion crescents.
- **C cheese wedge (pun):** 0.2 s a first hole pops in it; 1-3 s it thickens to a golden wedge with a rind and more holes pop; 4 s a mouse pokes out of the biggest hole.

**Pick: B, the bat.** The hanging point is already a folded wing, so the shape becomes an animal that could live under that bank (rule 1) and does something physical, unfolding and squeaking (rule 2). The drip is good own nature but quiet for a middle note; the cheese is a prop pun with a small face.
