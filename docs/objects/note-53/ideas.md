hat on the orange sprite's yellow head (the starfish-butterfly-jellyfish sprite of pedal sustain), wedged against the

- **B, hat tip (character):** 0.2 s the cap lifts off the head a crack / 1-3 s it tips up in greeting in three

  tip and curl over the slope, one colour after another / 4 s a last spray of fat confetti blobs.

**Pick: A, with C's popper as a small last beat.** The cap is already a hat, so it grows more of itself (rule 1) and
turns festive (rule 3): taller, striped, then the pom-pom and a short burst of two or three streamers at 4 s. B has no
hand to lift the hat with and reads odd. Keep it to the left of the spike so it stays clear of the sprite's own
jellyfish animation.
