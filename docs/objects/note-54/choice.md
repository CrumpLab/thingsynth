# note-54 F#3: choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A ruff (the ring fans out into a tiered ruff and a frilled-lizard collar), B hula (the ring
swings out round the cape as a hoop), C donut (icing, drips, sprinkles, a bite).

Picked A, the ruff. The ring changes in its own nature, a collar becoming more of a collar, and it builds big, heavy
and slow, tier by tier, as a low note should. The hoop is a prop and its swing would crowd the waving collar arm below.
The donut would be party food again after the banana split.
