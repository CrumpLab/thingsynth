# note-54 F#3 (f288)

**The shape:** the translucent hot-pink ring (240x162) round the neck of the tall horned mask at the left edge, just
under its red lips, sitting on top of the pale pink cape (the collar); the mask's mint flipper and the green
slit-eyed blob are below it. A neck ring, nothing more: no face or limbs of its own.

- **A ruff (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s: the ring's front edge buckles into a first lumpy candy scallop; 1-3 s: a
  pink ruff fans out round the neck tier by tier (pink, then lilac, then butter, each wider and more ruffled);
  4 s: the outer tier flips out into a huge frilled-lizard collar (~550 px) framing the mask's chin.
- **B hula (character action):** 0.2 s: the ring slips a little down the neck and tilts; 1-3 s: it swings out round
  the cape as a big hoop in three poses, left, front, right, a pink sash of motion behind it; 4 s: it swings
  widest with candy motion crescents either side, as if the mask were hula-ing.
- **C donut (pun on "ring"):** 0.2 s: a thick fold of glossy magenta icing bulges up on the ring; 1-3 s: icing
  drips run down over the cape one by one and candy sprinkles pop on; 4 s: it is a fat frosted donut with a bite
  taken out of its side.

**Pick: A, the ruff.** The ring itself changes in its own nature (rule 1), a collar becoming more of a collar, and
it is the one idea that builds big, heavy and slow as a low note should, tier after tier to a big payoff. The hula
hoop is a prop and its swing crowds the waving collar arm below; the donut is a good pun but party food again
after the banana split.
