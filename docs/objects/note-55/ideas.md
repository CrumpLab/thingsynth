# note-55 G3 (f170)

**What it is:** the hot-pink rounded body of the little horned figure standing on the grey ground, bottom centre: a tall thimble shape (about 140x215) with a green almond eye under a heavy lid line, two yellow horns, and a flared purple skirt at its foot; the purple path's tail ends between its horns, and the pedal split's sundae rises behind it to the right.

- **A lighthouse (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s its top lifts into a lamp cap; 1-3 s candy bands climb its body, the eye lights up as a lamp; 4 s two fat candy beams swing out from it.
- **B cheering (character):** 0.2 s its lid lifts; 1-3 s stubby pink arms rise out of its sides one then the other, a mouth opens; 4 s both arms up with motion crescents.
- **C ballerina (celebration):** 0.2 s its purple skirt flicks out at the hem; 1-3 s the skirt ruffles out into a big layered tutu, pink arms rise into a ring over its horns; 4 s it is up on a pointed toe mid-pirouette with swirl crescents round it.

**Pick: C, the ballerina.** Its most characterful part, the skirt, changes in its own form into a tutu (rule 1), the figure does something physical and festive (rules 2 and 3), and it avoids another eye opening. The lighthouse is a prop-like morph; the cheering arms repeat many waves already built.
