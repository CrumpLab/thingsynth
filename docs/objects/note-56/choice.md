# note-56 G#3 (f066): choice

Offered: **A** toffee drip (the point sags into a long glossy drip that drops into a puddle on the froth), **B**
Pinocchio (a sleepy eye opens on the brown panel and a wooden nose grows out of it, ending in a leafy twig), **C**
uncorked (the point is a bottle neck pouring into the foam, which overflows with bubbles and streamers).

Picked **B, Pinocchio**. The panel's own wood does the growing (5a rule 1) and it becomes a character with a face
doing something playful (rule 2). The heavy, slow push suits a low note. The drip was the runner-up but quieter, and
the bottle read as a prop.
