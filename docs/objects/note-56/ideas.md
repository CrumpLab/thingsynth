# note-56 G#3 (f066): ideas

**What it is:** the left brown panel (#A77360, opaque, 127x241) of the brown trunk hanging under the orange-brimmed
cap with the pale blue band and red mouth, bottom centre-right; it tapers to a hooked point that sinks into the white
froth below, with the cream long-eared creature on its left and the purple trunk creature's striped head below.

- **A Toffee drip (own nature):** the point is a drip of brown toffee. 0.2 s: the tip sags and fattens into a bead;
  1-3 s: it stretches down over the froth into a long glossy drip with a second, shorter drip beside it and a
  caramel-gold shine running down them; 4 s: the fat bulb lets go a big drop that lands in a lumpy toffee puddle on the
  froth.
- **B Pinocchio (character):** the brown panel is wood, and it is a face. 0.2 s: a round eye pops open half-lidded
  near its top; 1-3 s: its point pushes out slow and heavy, a lumpy wooden nose growing down-left over the froth, a
  knot and a grain stripe arriving on it, the eye opening wider in surprise; 4 s: a green twig with two leaves sprouts
  from the nose's tip (about 450 px long in all).
- **C Uncorked (celebration / pun on the froth):** the brown point is the neck of a bottle pouring into the white
  foam. 0.2 s: a gold foil collar wraps the neck; 1-3 s: the foam under it surges up in lumpy white puffs, a candy
  label band appears on the panel, fat bubbles rise round it; 4 s: the foam overflows in a big frothy crown with three
  candy streamers curling out of it.

**Pick: B, Pinocchio.** The panel's own form does the growing (5a rule 1) and it becomes a character with a face doing
something playful (rule 2): a low note gets a big, heavy, slow-building push of wood. The drip is a fine runner-up but
quieter; the bottle reads as a prop.
