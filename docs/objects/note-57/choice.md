# note-57 A3 (f216): choice

**Offered:** A pea pod (the lobe splits along its seam and three peas pop up), B balancing seal (the lobe rears up
into a pink seal that balances a beach ball on its nose and claps), C party horn (it stretches into a striped horn
blowing a streamer and confetti).

**Picked: B, the balancing seal.** The lobe turns into an animal that could live there (the narwhal pattern) and does
a playful, physical trick, drawn big up into the open orange face of the pyramid, clear of its two neighbour lobes.
The pod was left to the rest of the keyboard; the horn is a prop.
