# note-57 A3 (f216)

**The shape:** the middle of three petal-like lobes fanning from the top right of the acorn frog's round lime back
(f216, pink #E475A3, a tilted lozenge about 120 x 205 px leaning up and to the right, one thin ink stroke), with the
upright coral lobe (note-80) on its left, the magenta lobe (note-60) on its right, the tunnel's dark teal arch behind
it and the orange pyramid face above.

- **A pea pod (own nature: the lobe is a pod).** 0.2 s: its seam cracks open at the tip; 1-3 s: the two halves part
  and three fat green peas swell up out of it one a second; 4 s: the last, biggest pea pops up out of the pod with a
  pale shine.
- **B balancing seal (character).** The lobe becomes a pink seal reared up to the upper right. 0.2 s: its tip
  rounds into a snout with a dark nose; 1-3 s: an eye opens, a front flipper swings out, a candy-striped ball drops
  onto its nose; 4 s: the ball's stripes fill in and the other flipper claps up beside it.
- **C party horn (celebration).** The lobe stretches into a candy-striped party horn. 0.2 s: its tip flares into a
  bell; 1-3 s: bands of pink and yellow ring it, a curled paper streamer shoots out of the bell; 4 s: a burst of fat
  candy confetti blobs.

**Pick: B, the balancing seal.** Under 5a the lobe morphs into an animal that could live there (rule 1, the narwhal
pattern) and does a playful, physical trick (rule 2), drawn big (the seal about 280 px, the ball 120 px) up into the
open pyramid face, clear of its two neighbours. The pod is a good own-nature idea but the brief's own example is a
pod, so the keyboard will likely have one already; the horn is a prop.
