# note-58 A#3: f230, the yellow face of the leaning spike

**What it is:** a long narrow bright yellow wedge (#FFDE00, opaque, ~280x325), the lit face of the orange spike that
leans up and left beside the pyramid, pointing to a sharp tip near the mushroom stalks, with the pink sky cloud
behind it and the straw and the pea-pod sliver (f264) along its left.

- **A, daffodil (own-nature):** 0.2 s the tip splits a crack like a bud's sheath / 1-3 s yellow petals peel open
  from it one after another into a big lumpy daffodil / 4 s the orange trumpet pushes out of the middle with a
  frilled rim.
- **B, Pinocchio (character):** 0.2 s a sleepy eye opens near its base / 1-3 s the tip grows longer in two jumps,
  lumpy, like a nose / 4 s a leaf sprouts on the end of it.
- **C, pencil (pun on the painting's own pencil scribbles):** 0.2 s the yellow face turns into a pencil's sharpened
  wood, a dark lead tip appearing / 1-3 s a pink eraser band pops on, then a fat candy scribble ribbon loops out
  from the lead across the pink cloud / 4 s the scribble closes into a loop-the-loop.

**Pick: A, daffodil.** A pointed yellow sheath opening into a flower is the spike changing in its own nature (rule 1)
and living (rule 5), with one big petal a second and the trumpet as the payoff. C is the runner-up and the most
distinctive (it plays on the painting's own scribbles), but it is a mechanical prop; B is a joke on a face.
