# note-59 B3 (f244): ideas

**What it is.** A see-through tan-pink almond lobe (salmon #F3897B at 46%, 132x209) hanging point-down on the chest of
the waving pink creature, lower left: under its snout with the green spike, left of its raised mitten hand, with the
party narwhal's lavender-teal mitten arm crossing just below it; it reads as the creature's lowered second paw or a
pouch on its belly.

- **A, own-nature morph: piñata.** It hangs like one. 0.2 s: a first candy band (pink) and a paper fringe flick out
  across its top. 1-3 s: it swells to about 2x, banded pink, yellow, cyan, lime, with fringed edges, and a
  cracked seam opens across its belly. 4 s: it bursts at the seam and a spill of chunky candies tumbles out under it.
- **B, character: joey in the pouch.** The lobe is a pouch. 0.2 s: its rim lifts open into a dark slit. 1-3 s: two
  long ears poke out, then a little head with sleepy eyes rises, the eyes open. 4 s: a small paw comes up out of the
  pouch and waves.
- **C, celebration pun: clap along.** It is the creature's other paw. 0.2 s: it twitches up off its chest. 1-3 s: it
  swings up in poses to meet the raised mitten. 4 s: the two hands clap together with clap crescents and a few candy
  confetti pieces.

**Pick: A, the piñata.** The hanging almond changes in its own nature into something that could hang there (rule 1),
and it is the most celebratory of the three (rule 3): candy bands, fringe, and the burst landing exactly on the 4 s
payoff, which suits a lower note's heavy, slow build. The joey is a sweet character but repeats the many waking
eyes nearby; the clap needs a sprite arm to rotate through poses and crowds the raised mitten.
