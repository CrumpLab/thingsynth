# note-60 C4 (f217): choice

**Offered:** A snapdragon (the lobe is a petal: its lips part on a yellow throat and it sings), B "Do, a doe" (middle
C is Do: a doe's head with ears, spots and an opening eye), C kiss (it puckers into lips and blows hearts).

**Picked: A, the singing snapdragon.** The petal turns into the flower it belongs to and does something with it: its
mouth opens and sings, a reading of a sounding note. It needs no new eye, which keeps eyes a minority in this
corner. The doe was a fun pun but another face; the kiss was mostly hearts added round it.
