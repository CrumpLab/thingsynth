# note-60 C4 (f217)

**The shape:** the rightmost of the three lobes fanning from the acorn frog's lime back (f217, magenta #E475D4, a
rounded leaf about 200 x 190 px pointing right towards the coral tongue, its stem end tucked in under the pink lobe
note-57), with the frog's lower acorn below it and the dark tunnel and the orange pyramid face behind.

- **A snapdragon (own nature: the lobe is a petal).** 0.2 s: its tip parts a crack into two lips; 1-3 s: the lips
  open wide on a yellow throat, two more magenta petals unfurl above and below; 4 s: it sings, a round open mouth
  with a pale tongue and two fat song arcs to the right.
- **B Do, a doe (literal pun: middle C is Do).** 0.2 s: a dark nose pops on its tip; 1-3 s: a big ear swings up,
  a heavy-outlined almond eye opens sleepy then wide, two cream spots; 4 s: a second ear and a little tuft pop, the
  doe's head looking out over the tongue.
- **C kiss (celebration).** The lobe puckers into big magenta lips. 0.2 s: a pucker crease; 1-3 s: the lips round
  out, a fat pink heart puffs out of them, then a second; 4 s: a third, biggest heart in red with a shine.

**Pick: A, the snapdragon.** The lobe changes in its own nature (rule 1: a petal becoming the flower it belongs to)
and does something (it opens its mouth and sings; rule 2), a fitting reading of a sounding note (rule 4), and it
needs no eye, keeping eyes a minority in the batch. The doe is a fun pun but adds another face; the kiss is mostly
hearts added round it.
