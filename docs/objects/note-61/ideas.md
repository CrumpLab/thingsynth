**What it is:** the thin waterfall (#ACF9FF at 0.57): a pale cyan stream about 40 px wide that arcs from the pink
flower creature's pale snout up, over and down into the pool, landing in a little ring of water by the white froth;
leafy green tufts stand behind it and the tiny green tuft (note-102) sits at its spout.

- **A, own nature (loop-the-loop):** 0.2 s the stream's foot kicks up out of the pool in a kink; 1-3 s the water

  arch on its belly, one pose a second, arms out; 4 s it hits the pool in a big splash crown, waving.
- **C, celebration (water balloon):** 0.2 s the stream's foot swells into a little bulb; 1-3 s it fills into a fat
  wobbly candy-pink water balloon, stretching bigger and bigger as the water keeps pouring in; 4 s it bursts in a
  starburst of splash and drops.

playful and physical, and its first beat lifts straight out of the pool for a tap. The otter is a visitor rather than
the water; the balloon is a swell-and-pop that leans on the balloon animal and narwhal fountain. Note: a spiral-like
idea, so check it against the tunnel rim's curl; loops kept over the pool, clear of the frogs on the bank.
