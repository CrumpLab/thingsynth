# note-62 D4 (f233)

**The shape:** the hot magenta wedge at the bottom of the tall two-tone blue horned ghost (lower drawbar 6), centre
(f233, #FF1ABE, about 160 x 160 px), split by a curved stroke and lipped by a black curl at its left: it reads as the
ghost's open mouth with its tongue in it, at its hem, with the green cat-eared creature and its blue pill below.

- **A long tongue (own nature).** 0.2 s: the tongue tip pokes out over the lip; 1-3 s: it rolls out longer, down and
  to the left, a fat candy tongue curling at its end; 4 s: it curls up into a full loop with a lick-shine.
- **B bubblegum (character).** 0.2 s: a pink nub of gum pokes out of the mouth; 1-3 s: it swells into a bubble,
  then a bigger one, its cheek puffing; 4 s: the biggest bubble (about 260 px) with a white shine and a smaller
  bubble budding off its side.
- **C party blower (celebration).** 0.2 s: a striped mouthpiece pops into its mouth; 1-3 s: the paper tongue unrolls
  out to the left in stages, banded pink and yellow; 4 s: it is out straight with a feather tassel and a little puff of
  confetti.

**Pick: B, bubblegum.** The ghost's own mouth doing something playful and physical (rule 2), a big round candy shape
the size of the painting's own (it was offered for the snout but never built), and every second of the hold has
something new to see: the gum, a first bubble, a bigger one, the shine. The tongue echoes the coral tongue's fork;
the blower is a prop.
