# note-63 D#4 (f085): ideas

**What it is:** the pink stub (#F681D9), a rounded pink peg about 115 x 270 px standing up out of a pale blue cap on a
peach cone under the purple ledge's hem, like a candle on a cupcake or a lipstick in its tube; the cyan ring
(note-68) is to its left and the orange capsule to its right.

- **A, own nature (lipstick):** 0.2 s the pink bullet twists up a notch out of its tube; 1-3 s it rises in three
  twists, taller and slanted at the tip, a glossy shine running up it; 4 s a big pink kiss print (lumpy lips) smacks
  onto the air beside it.
- **B, character (periscope worm):** 0.2 s the stub's tip bends over; 1-3 s it rises as a neck, a head swells on top
  and looks left, then right; 4 s it stretches up tall and opens a round mouth to sing.
- **C, celebration (birthday cupcake):** 0.2 s a flame lights on the stub's tip; 1-3 s candy stripes wrap the candle,
  sprinkles pop onto the blue frosting and a cherry-sized swirl of frosting puffs up round the candle; 4 s the flame
  flares tall with a puff of candy sparks.

**Pick: C, birthday cupcake.** It is what the shape already looks like, turned into a party (rules 3 and 4), with a
flame that lights instantly for a tap and builds to a payoff. The lipstick is a good pun but a prop; the periscope
worm is a face on a stalk with little to do.
