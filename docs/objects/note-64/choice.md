# note-64 E4 (f102): choice

**Offered:** A hatching (the yellow almond is an egg in the cap's nest; a chick hatches and cheeps); B mouse in the cheese (holes open, a mouse nibbles); C rubber duck (the almond is a rubber duck's back afloat in the cap; its head rises, suds puff up, it squeaks).

**Picked: C, rubber duck** (picked centrally; the hatching chick went to note 73). It is a playful, celebratory bath-time morph of the shape: the almond becomes the duck's own body, and the squeak is a bright, quick payoff for E4.

**Built:** a pointed yellow tail flicks up from the almond's point (0-0.06). The round head rises out from behind its back (0.1-0.3): the almond is laid again in front, so the neck tucks behind it. The orange bill (0.3-0.42) and a round eye (0.4-0.5) arrive, then a lumpy wing on its back (0.45-0.6). White suds puff up one after another along its waterline (0.55-0.88), drawn as an outlined layer under a fill-only layer so they melt into one foam. At the end the lower bill drops open and two fat cyan and pink squeak crescents go out (0.84-1). Everything is `over`.
