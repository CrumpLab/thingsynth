# note-64 E4 (f102): ideas

**The shape:** a butter-yellow almond lobe, about 150 px across, at the right end of the upper orange cap that the pink one-eyed finger figure stands on. Its point is tucked under a black tick at the cap's top. The cyan creature's head is below it, and the mauve gap (note-76) sits just below its right end. No face or limbs.

- **A, hatching (own nature):** the yellow almond is an egg sitting in the orange cap as a nest. 0.2 s a zigzag crack pops across it; 1-3 s the top shell lifts off and tips aside, and a fluffy yellow chick head (about 180 px) pushes up out of it with a tuft on top; 4 s the chick's stubby wings fling up and its orange beak opens in a cheep.
- **B, mouse in the cheese (character):** the lobe is a wedge of cheese. 0.2 s a first round hole pops open in it; 1-3 s two more holes open, a lilac mouse's pink nose and then its big round ears push out of the biggest hole; 4 s its whole head is out, nibbling, and a bite-shaped chunk is gone from the wedge's tip.
- **C, rubber duck (celebration):** the lobe is a duck's yellow back floating in the cap as a tub. 0.2 s a curled tail flicks up at its end; 1-3 s a round yellow head and a fat orange bill rise out of the cap, and suds puff up round it; 4 s it squeaks, with two fat sound crescents.

**Pick: A, hatching.** The shape changes in its own nature into something that could live there (rule 1): a pale almond in an orange cup is an egg in a nest. A chick arriving and cheeping is friendly and celebratory (rules 2 and 3), and it is bright and quick enough for E4. B and C bring a visitor or a toy in from outside.
