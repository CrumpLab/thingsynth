# note-65 F4 (f057): choice

**Offered:**
- A, heart eye: the eye white swells into a lumpy pink heart that beats.
- B, batting lashes: long candy lashes curl out and the lid flutters.
- C, starry-eyed: the white becomes a big lumpy star.

**Picked: A, the heart eye.** The eye itself morphs, and lovestruck gives the bunny a feeling. It reads as one bold
shape on a small note. It also doesn't repeat the voice.manuals hippo build, which wakes this bunny with an open eye.
B's lashes risk being thin and fiddly, and C is closer to decoration.

**Built:**
- By 0.2 s the eye white has gone and a pink heart about the eye's size sits in its place.
- From 0.6 s to 1.1 s it swells past the lid.
- From 1.6 s to 2.4 s comes the first beat: it swells bigger and sinks back.
- From 2.6 s to 3 s the second beat stays, at about 240 px. A pale inner heart arrives with it, and a white glint
  follows.
- From 3.4 s to 4 s two little hearts pop off it, up and to the right.

The eye white fades out as the first heart lands (vanish), and the hearts are placed over.
