# note-65 F4 (f057): ideas

**What it is:** the white of the magenta bunny's big sleepy eye, a tall pale cyan oval about 120 x 190 px with faint
streaks. It sits under the red half-closed lid, with lash ticks on top, on the bunny's head at the right of the
bunny slab. (The hippo build for voice.manuals already wakes this bunny on Upper, with a tall open eye over this one.)

- **A, own-nature morph: heart eye.** At 0.2 s a pink blush floods the white and its bottom dips into a point. Over
  1-3 s it swells into a big lumpy pink heart that pushes out past the lid and beats bigger twice. At 4 s two small
  hearts pop off it, up and to the right.
- **B, character action: batting lashes.** At 0.2 s the first long curled lash sweeps up off the lid. Over 1-3 s three
  more fat candy lashes curl out one by one and the lid dips in a slow flutter. At 4 s all the lashes are up, with a
  wink sparkle.
- **C, celebration: starry-eyed.** At 0.2 s a yellow point pokes out of the white. Over 1-3 s it swells into a lumpy
  five-point star bigger than the eye, with a pink rim. At 4 s it glints and two little stars pop beside it.

**Pick: A, the heart eye.** The eye itself morphs into something else (5a rule 1), and lovestruck is the character
feeling something playful (rule 2). It reads as one bold shape on a small note. It also does not repeat the hippo
build's eye opening. B's lashes risk being thin and fiddly, and C is closer to decoration.
