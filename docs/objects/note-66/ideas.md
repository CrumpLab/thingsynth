# note-66 F#4 (f013): ideas

**What it is.** A solid red-pink (#F64C65) drop or tongue, 204x184, hanging at the bottom right of the striped stack
(pink cap with its purple eye, deep pink, purple, then this red) beside the sleepy green creature, with a little ink
curl hooked inside it; it sits on the painting's bottom edge, the lavender ground and yellow baguette arm to its
left and the grey lying creature to its right.

- **A, own-nature morph: strawberry.** 0.2 s: the first pale seeds pop on it. 1-3 s: more seeds, it swells plump and
  lumpy, a green leafy crown fans out on its top edge. 4 s: a white strawberry blossom pops open beside the crown.
- **B, character: ladybug.** 0.2 s: a big black spot pops on its back. 1-3 s: more spots, a black head with two
  knobbed antennae bumps out on its right, its shell splits down the middle and the two halves lift. 4 s: two
  see-through wings fan out from under the shell, with motion crescents: it is about to take off.
- **C, celebration pun: party blower.** Its inside curl is the rolled-up blower. 0.2 s: the tip pokes out to the
  right. 1-3 s: it unrolls across the grey creature in stretches, candy stripes coming on as it goes. 4 s: fully out,
  a fringed tassel at its tip and two toot puffs.

**Pick: B, the ladybug.** The red drop morphs into a small creature that could live there (rule 1), and it does
something physical and friendly, opening its shell and wings for take-off (rule 2), with a clear first beat (the
spot) at a tap. The party blower is the runner-up (its own curl unrolling, celebratory), the better choice if the
keyboard already has enough bugs; the strawberry is a pleasant morph with no action.
