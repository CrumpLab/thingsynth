# note-67 G4 (f168): ideas

**The shape:** a small see-through pale aqua patch (#94EBF5, 208x88, no ink of its own) of distant hill or pond on the
horizon, between the orange capsule with its eye-hole and the coral hand's antler, its top edge a wavy little hill line
under the purple ledge's frilly hem.

- **A. Bluebells (own-nature morph):** the little hillside sprouts. 0.2 s: a first green shoot pokes up from its hill
  line. 1-3 s: two more shoots rise and arch over (1), aqua-blue bells swell on their tips (2), the bells open with
  frilled lips (3). 4 s: they ring, small candy sound crescents flicking out round them.
- **B. Duckling (character):** the patch is a pond. 0.2 s: a little yellow head bobs up out of it with a splash ring.
  1-3 s: its body and tail pop up (1), an eye opens (2), its orange beak opens cheeping (3). 4 s: its wings flick up
  and a second duckling pops up beside it.
- **C. Bunting (celebration):** 0.2 s: a first candy pennant pops down from the capsule's side. 1-3 s: pennants pop
  one after another on a ribbon across the gap toward the antler, stripes on each. 4 s: the last one lands on the
  antler and a little rosette pops on top.

**Pick: A, bluebells.** For a high note: small, bright and quick, a sprout that rings (the note sounding drawn as the
thing in its own nature), on its own hill line, without another face in a corner that already has eyes. B is a sweet
character but adds another eye; C is decoration.
