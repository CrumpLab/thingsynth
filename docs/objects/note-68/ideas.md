# note-68 G#4 (f080): ideas

**What it is:** the cyan ring (#48DAFF), a fat tilted torus about 170 x 220 px at the end of a khaki tube, a purple
plug pushed through its hole from the left (the purple path's right arm ends against it), on the pool's lower shore
just left of the cupcake stub (note-63).

- **A, own nature (pool float):** 0.2 s the ring puffs up fatter with a shine; 1-3 s candy stripes come round it one
  by one as it inflates into a swim ring, a duck's neck rises from its top; 4 s the duck head pops on, a pink beak
  open in a quack: a duck pool float by the pool.
- **B, character (bubblegum lips):** 0.2 s the ring's rim puckers into pink lips round the purple plug; 1-3 s the
  purple gum swells out of them into a bubble, bigger each second; 4 s the biggest bubble, with a white shine and a
  second lumpy bubble on its side.
- **C, pun (ring with a gem):** 0.2 s the ring blushes gold at its top; 1-3 s it turns into a gold band and the purple
  plug swells into a big lumpy amethyst set on prongs; 4 s glints flash on the gem.

**Pick: A, pool float.** The ring changes in its own nature into a thing that lives by the pool (rule 1), gets a
friendly face that quacks (rule 2) and is a pool party (rule 3). Bubblegum is the runner-up (a fine swell for a hold)
but its bubble sits over the snake's head on the purple path; the gem repeats the cliff's crystals.
