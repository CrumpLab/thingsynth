# note-69 A4 (f056)

**What it is:** the thin dusty-pink right ear (71x350) of the magenta bunny that rises from the orange pot on the
bunny slab, bottom right: a tall rounded pill with an inner line, standing beside the bunny's broader left ear,
under the big-eye creature's red lip.

- **A, own-nature morph (lop ear):** 0.2 s its tip bends over / 1-3 s the ear stretches taller and flops over to
  the right, folding at the middle / 4 s the long ear hangs down in a loose curl with a pink inner lining showing.
- **B, character (waving ear):** 0.2 s the ear's tip twitches forward / 1-3 s it bends at the middle and waves
  side to side, each pose a beat / 4 s a mitten-like tip waves high with two motion crescents.
- **C, pun (ear of corn):** 0.2 s the first plump yellow kernels pop up at its base / 1-3 s kernels fill it row by
  row up to the tip while green husk leaves unfurl from its foot / 4 s a full ear of corn, husks open wide and a
  pink-cream silk tassel bursting from its tip.

**Pick: C, ear of corn.** A bunny's ear turning into an ear of corn is a playful literal pun drawn as a big object in
its own long shape (5a rules 1 and 4), with a steady arrival every second (kernels by rows, husks, silk) that suits a
middle note. The lop ear is runner-up but is only a bend; the wave repeats the many waving limbs already built.
