# note-70 A#4: choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A calla lily (the tube's end flares into a calla trumpet with a spadix), B hermit crab (eye
stalks, claws and a wave out of the tube), C birthday candle (wick, flame, stripes, wax drips).

Picked A, the calla lily (made centrally; the hermit crab went to note 45). The tube's open end blooms in its own
nature, and a calla is itself a tube with a flared lip, so it grows out of the painted shape without a seam.
