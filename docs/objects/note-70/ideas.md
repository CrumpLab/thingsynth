# note-70 A#4 (f267)

**The shape:** the pale yellow oval (137x154) at the upper-left end of a stubby pink tube that leans up-left out of
the lavender dish, middle left; it reads as the open end of the tube (or a cut face), with the lilac bell on its
left and the cyan-scribbled pod above it. No face or limbs.

- **A calla lily (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s: the oval's rim curls back at one edge as a first petal lip;
  1-3 s: the tube flares open into a big white-and-pink calla trumpet, its lip rolling back round; 4 s: a fat
  yellow spadix rises out of the middle with a dusting of pollen puffs.
- **B hermit crab (character action):** 0.2 s: two eye-stalks pop up out of the oval; 1-3 s: the eyes open
  (sleepy, then wide), a candy-red claw and then two legs reach out of the tube; 4 s: the big claw lifts and
  waves hello, the tube now its shell.
- **C birthday candle (celebration):** 0.2 s: a wick pokes up out of the oval; 1-3 s: a small flame catches and
  grows, candy stripes wind up the tube and wax drips run down; 4 s: the flame flares tall with a glow and three
  sparks.

**Pick: B, the hermit crab.** The tube already reads as a shell, so a crab coming out of it is company arriving in
the object's own terms (rules 1 and 2), friendly and physical, with the wave as the payoff, each part 120-250 px.
The lily is a pleasant morph but another flower in a painting full of plants; the candle is a decoration on a
cone.
