# note-71 B4 (f283)

**What it is:** a slate-blue four-sided patch (purple #81418B at 0.51 over the teal wash, 126x133) in the shade
right under the grey-green cap's rim, boxed between the dark left stalk and the pale middle stalk, with the cream gap
of note-24 running down below it.

- **A, honey hive (morph + "B for bee" pun):** the shady pocket under the cap swells into a hive. 0.2 s: a lumpy
  golden bulge pushes out of the patch. 1-3 s: it fattens into a banded, hand-drawn hive, a dark door opens, a honey
  drip runs down. 4 s: two fat candy bees zip out of the door on a looping trail.
- **B, the bat wakes (morph + character):** the patch is a bat hanging folded in the shade under the cap. 0.2 s:
  two pointed ears flick down out of its lower corners (it hangs upside down). 1-2 s: sleepy eyes open, a little
  fanged smile. 2-4 s: its wings unfold wide either side over the stalks, ribbed and scalloped in candy violet, a
  squeak burst last.
- **C, party lantern (celebration):** the patch becomes a paper lantern hanging from the cap. 0.2 s: a hanging cord
  and a red top rim appear. 1-3 s: it swells into a lumpy candy-ribbed lantern, its inside lights gold, a tassel
  drops. 4 s: it sways and a second smaller lantern pops on beside it.

**Pick: B, the bat.** The dark patch itself becomes something living that hangs exactly where it is (rule 1), wakes and
spreads its wings (rule 2), and the squeak is a high-middle note; the hive is a pun with a visitor, the lantern a prop.
