# note-72 C5 (f284)

**What it is:** a small rounded mauve pod (#81418B at 0.51, 99x104, reads purple over the teal) hanging under the
grey-green cap's rim just right of the pale middle stalk, the cream gap of the caterpillar column below it.

- **A, seed pod bursts (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s: a seam splits down the pod's middle with a pale glint. 1-3 s:
  its two halves curl back like peel, and three round candy seeds pop out one a second, each bouncing out on its
  stalk. 4 s: the seeds open little mouths and sing, a fluff tuft on each.
- **B, hanging dormouse (character):** the pod is a curled purple dormouse hanging by its tail under the cap. 0.2 s:
  a round ear pops out. 1-3 s: its eye opens, it uncurls, a paw and a second ear come out. 4 s: it swings
  upright, waving, its long tail coiled round the stalk.
- **C, piñata (celebration):** the hanging pod is a piñata. 0.2 s: a ruffled candy fringe pops round its foot.
  1-3 s: it swings, more fringe tiers in pink, yellow, cyan, and a crack opens. 4 s: it bursts and candy
  (fat wrapped sweets and confetti blobs) tumbles out below.

**Pick: A, the seed pod.** The pod does the most pod-like thing (rule 1), the singing seeds give it characters
(rule 2), and the arrivals spread evenly over the hold; the piñata is a fine party pun but a prop, the dormouse
adds a visitor's face. Watch the hooray left arm of Upper 8', which rises near here.
