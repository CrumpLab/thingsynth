# note-73 C#5 (f017): ideas

**What it is.** A see-through pale yellow wedge (#FFF9AD at 56%, 154x180, reads pinkish-tan over the wash) pointing
up, a negative-space gap standing on top of the striped stack's pink cap, between the cyan creature's orange brim on
its left and the narwhal's lavender-teal mitten arm on its right, the waving pink creature's almond lobe (note-59)
just above.

- **A, own-nature morph: cheese wedge.** It is a pale yellow wedge. 0.2 s: the first round hole pops in it.
  1-3 s: it swells into a chunky lumpy wedge of cheese, more holes popping. 4 s: a pink mouse nose and whiskers
  poke out of the biggest hole.
- **B, character: hatching chick.** Egg-coloured and pointed like a shell's tip. 0.2 s: a zigzag crack splits across
  it. 1-3 s: its top lifts off as a shell cap, a fluffy yellow chick head pushes up with sleepy eyes, the eyes open.
  4 s: its orange beak opens wide in a chirp, the shell cap tipped on its head.
- **C, celebration pun: birthday candle.** The striped stack under it is a layer cake. 0.2 s: a wick flame lights on
  its tip. 1-3 s: it stretches into a candy-striped candle, the flame swells, a wax drip runs. 4 s: the flame flares
  with a ring of small sparks.

**Pick: B, the hatching chick.** The pale wedge changes into something living that could be there (rule 1), and a
character arrives and does a bright, physical thing, the chirp, which suits a high-middle note (rule 2); the crack
is a strong tap beat. The candle is a good pun but a prop on top; the cheese leans clip-art. Note: the hare of
percussion decay springs its ears up over the pink cap's right half, right here; the two rarely play together.
