# note-74 D5 (f203): ideas

**What it is:** the dark wedge (#602D3B at 0.68), a shadowy maroon triangle about 230 x 145 px tucked in the hollow
under the dark bush's ragged foot, its long edge on the pink band that runs off to the right, the hedge (note-31) just
to its left: a dark little hollow or cave mouth.

- **A, own nature (bat):** 0.2 s a pointed ear pricks up from its top corner; 1-3 s a scalloped maroon wing unfolds
  out of each side, a second ear, a little face with a pink nose; 4 s the wings stretch wide and its mouth opens in a
  tiny fanged squeak.
- **B, character (mole):** 0.2 s a pink star nose pokes out of the dark; 1-3 s the head and two big digging paws
  push out, clods of earth flying; 4 s the mole sits up out of its hole waving a paw.
- **C, pun (cake slice):** 0.2 s a cream layer stripes across the wedge; 1-3 s it becomes a slice of chocolate cake
  lying on its side, pink frosting dripping over its edge, layers of cream; 4 s a fat cherry lands on its point.

**Pick: A, bat.** The dark wedge itself turns into the creature that lives in a dark hollow (rule 1), in its own
maroon, with wings that unfold one per beat and a small, quick squeak for a higher note. The mole is a visitor; the
cake is a third party food next to the cupcake and lies flat.
