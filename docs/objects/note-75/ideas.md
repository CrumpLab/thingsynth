# note-75 D#5 (f205)

**The shape:** the pale sky-blue pill held up in the raised arm of the sage-green, cat-eared creature under the horned
ghost, centre (f205, #8FD5FF, about 110 x 185 px, no ink of its own: it lies in the arm's outline), with a lime nub
under it like a cup or handle, so it reads like a torch or an ice pop held high.

- **A ice pop (own nature).** 0.2 s: a bite notch pops out of its top; 1-3 s: it glazes with a white frost shine and
  fat blue drips run down over the creature's hand; 4 s: a second bite and a pink candy stripe show inside.
- **B firefly (character).** The pill is a firefly's glowing tail. 0.2 s: its tip lights up yellow; 1-3 s: two
  clear lilac wings flick open above it, a little dark head with feelers pops out of its top, a ring of glow
  round it; 4 s: a second, bigger glow ring and the wings at full spread, as if about to lift off the hand.
- **C sparkler (celebration).** 0.2 s: a white-hot spark pops on its tip; 1-3 s: fat candy star sparks burst round
  it, more each second; 4 s: a full crown of sparks in yellow, pink and cyan.

**Pick: B, the firefly.** The pill turns into a small living thing that could be there (rule 1) with a character
to it (rule 2), and it is a high note's bright, quick light: the glow at the tap, then wings and feelers. The ice
pop is a fair own-nature idea but quieter; the sparkler is generic sparkle.
