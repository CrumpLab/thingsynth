# note-76 E5 (f016): ideas

**The shape:** a thin crescent-shaped sliver, about 230 x 100 px, between the two orange caps over the cyan creature's head. It is pale lemon at 56% opacity, so it reads dusty lilac-pink over the wash. The upper cap's yellow lobe (note-64) is above its left end, the lower-right orange cap is under it, and the cyan head is on its left. It is a gap, a crack between things.

- **A, crescent moon (own nature):** 0.2 s the sliver's right tip curls up and thickens; 1-3 s it fills into a fat lilac crescent moon lying on the cap, and a closed, smiling sleepy face (a long lashed lid curve and a smile, no eye opening) forms on its inner curve; 4 s three lumpy candy stars pop round it.
- **B, claws from the crack (character):** something lives in the crevice. 0.2 s a coral claw tip pokes up out of the crack; 1-3 s two big coral pincers rise up and to the right out of it, the first then the second, and their jaws open; 4 s they clack shut together, with two yellow motion crescents.
- **C, party blower (celebration):** the gap is a pair of puckered lips. 0.2 s they purse and a striped mouthpiece pokes out; 1-3 s a candy-striped party blower unrolls out of it to the right, a curl at a time; 4 s it is fully out with a fringed tip and a little puff of pink breath.

**Pick: B, claws from the crack.** It is physical and playful (rule 2): a crevice's own nature is something living in it, and the clack is a bright, quick payoff that suits E5. It grows up and right into the open mauve space, clear of note-64's egg. A is gentler but is a moon in the ground and adds another face. C is a prop.
