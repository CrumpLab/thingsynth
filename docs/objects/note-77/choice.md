# note-77 F5 (f300): choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A icicles (the pale cyan freezes), B the other eye (the band opens as the mask's missing
eye), C ribbon dance (the band peels off and ties a bow).

**Picked: B, the other eye.** The striped panel sits where the mask's second eye belongs, opposite its yellow almond
eye, so the mask's own anatomy wakes (rule 2); quick and bright for a high note. It is the batch's one eye idea.

Built: at a tap a magenta lid, shut with a lash line, appears over the pale wave; it lifts to a sleepy half lid (0.4-0.9
s) as a cyan iris and pupil look across at the yellow eye; the lid lifts wide into a fold above the eye (2-2.6 s);
three black lashes flick up one after another (2.7-3.4 s); a glint lands in the iris and a sparkle beside it (3.4-4 s).
A tall almond like the mask's own eye, drawn over the panel.
