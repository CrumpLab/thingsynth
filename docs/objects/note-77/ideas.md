# note-77 F5 (f300): ideas

**What it is.** The pale cyan wavy band (f300, 130x193) in the striped panel down the right side of the horned mask's
face, between a deep cyan stripe above and a magenta one below; the panel sits where the mask's second eye would be,
opposite its yellow almond eye.

- **A. Icicles (own-nature morph: pale cyan freezes).** 0.2 s: a frosty rim and a first icicle bud along its lower
  wave / 1-3 s: icicles lengthen one after another down over the magenta stripe / 4 s: the longest drips a drop and
  glints.
- **B. The other eye (character).** 0.2 s: the band parts along its wave like a lid / 1 s: the white shows / 2 s: a cyan
  iris and pupil look across at the yellow eye / 3 s: lashes flick up off the top wave / 4 s: wide open with a glint, the
  mask looking out with both eyes.
- **C. Ribbon dance (celebration).** 0.2 s: its right end lifts off the panel / 1-3 s: it peels away and flutters out to
  the right in loops / 4 s: it ties itself into a big floppy bow.

**Pick: B, the other eye.** It uses the mask's own anatomy (rule 2): the band is where the face's missing eye belongs,
so a quick, bright high note wakes it. This is the batch's one "eye opens" idea. Icicles echo the cliff's crystals and
the ribbon reads as decoration.
