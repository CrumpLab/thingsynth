# note-78 F#5 (f188)

**The shape:** the pink top of the pink-and-yellow egg hanging in the see-through lavender neck of the straw
creature, top right (f188, #FF75B4, a lumpy cap about 145 x 120 px over the yellow lower half), with the straw
crossing the neck above it and the apricot saucer below.

- **A cupcake (own nature: the pink cap is icing).** 0.2 s: a curl of pink frosting swirls up off its top; 1-3 s: the
  frosting piles up into a tall swirl, candy sprinkles pop on, a paper-case ridge shows on the yellow; 4 s: a
  cherry lands on top with a shine.
- **B hatching (character).** 0.2 s: a zigzag crack opens along its seam; 1-3 s: the pink shell-top lifts off and
  tips aside, a baby straw creature's tan head with round ears rises out; 4 s: its one eye opens wide and its mouth
  opens to peep.
- **C Faberge egg (celebration).** 0.2 s: a gold band snaps round its seam; 1-3 s: candy jewels pop on the pink one
  after another, gold filigree loops; 4 s: a jewelled crown with a pearl on top.

**Pick: B, hatching.** An egg doing what eggs do (rule 1) and company arriving (rule 2): the hatchling is a baby of
the straw creature itself (tan head, round ears, one eye), so it belongs in the picture. The cupcake is a good
party pun but a prop-like morph; the Faberge egg is decoration.
