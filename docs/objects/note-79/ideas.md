# note-79 G5 (f151)

**The shape:** the long pink bar (263x142) pointing up-right to a sharp tip, the diagonal arm of a pink cross that
sticks out of the blue ring-creature in the middle of the bottom left; the cyan cup sits at its root and the grey
lying creature's body is behind its tip. A spike or stick, no face.

- **A pencil (pun on the painting):** 0.2 s: the tip sharpens into a wood collar and a dark lead point; 1-3 s: the
  bar gains pencil facets and a pink eraser band at its root, and the point draws a fat candy squiggle (an outlined
  ribbon) out to the right; 4 s: the squiggle closes into a loop-the-loop.
- **B dragonfly (character, morph):** 0.2 s: two big round eyes pop on the tip; 1-3 s: two pairs of see-through
  candy wings unfold from behind the eyes, front pair then back pair, and segment bands come up along the bar;
  4 s: the wings lift, with motion crescents, as it buzzes.
- **C sparkler (celebration):** 0.2 s: the tip lights with a little white-gold spark; 1-3 s: the spark fizzes
  into a growing star burst and the stick darkens as it burns down from the tip; 4 s: a big spray of candy stars
  bursts round the tip.

**Pick: B, the dragonfly.** The long pointed bar already is a dragonfly's body, so the object becomes a creature
that could live there (rule 1) and does something lively (rule 2); its wings are big see-through shapes that read
at a glance and suit a bright, buzzing upper note. The pencil is a clever pun but a prop; the sparkler is plain
sparkles.
