# note-80 G#5 (f215): choice

**Offered:** A tulip (the nub opens into a coral tulip with a pistil and leaf), B lookout meerkat (it stretches up
into a meerkat on watch), C birthday candle (a flame lights on its tip and leaps up, stripes wind round the stub,
sparks pop).

**Picked: C, the birthday candle** (the keyboard's only candle). A high note's small, bright pop at the tap and a
celebratory reading of its own candle-stub form, like the pyramid's little flaming crown. The tulip would have sat
beside the snapdragon on the same frog, and the meerkat would add another waking face.
