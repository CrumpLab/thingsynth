# note-80 G#5 (f215)

**The shape:** the leftmost and smallest of the three lobes on the acorn frog's lime back (f215, coral #E48075, an
upright round-topped nub about 85 x 140 px, like a thumb or a candle stub), the tunnel's dark teal arch behind it,
the frog's upper acorn below-left and the pink lobe (note-57) leaning away on its right.

- **A tulip (own nature: the nub is a bud).** 0.2 s: its top notches into three points; 1-3 s: the points open out
  into a coral tulip, a yellow pistil rises, a green leaf curls off its foot; 4 s: two pollen puffs pop above it.
- **B lookout meerkat (character).** 0.2 s: the nub's top swells into a round head; 1-3 s: it stretches up into a
  coral meerkat standing tall, two stubby paws fold in, ears pop; 4 s: its eyes open and it looks round.
- **C birthday candle (celebration).** The nub is a candle stub. 0.2 s: a yellow flicker pops on its tip; 1-3 s:
  the flame leaps up taller, a white-hot heart and a blue foot light in it, pink candy stripes wind round the stub;
  4 s: a fat second tongue of flame and three sparks pop off its tip.

**Pick: C, the birthday candle.** A high note's small, bright and quick pop at the tap (the flame lights at once),
and a celebratory reading of its own form (the upright nub already reads as a candle stub; rules 3 and 4), like the
pyramid's little flaming crown. The tulip sits next to the snapdragon on the same frog (two flowers side by side),
and the meerkat adds yet another waking face.
