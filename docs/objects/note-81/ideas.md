# note-81 A5 (f063)

**What it is:** the small cream cap (199x87, no ink of its own) over a pale cyan pill lying under the right half of
the peach band (note-27), bottom right of centre, a curling black stroke running into it from the left; it reads
as an egg's top or the lid on a sleepy eye.

- **A, own-nature (eye wakes):** 0.2 s the cream lid lifts a crack / 1-3 s it rises, a round dark pupil looks up,
  three lashes flick out / 4 s it winks with a little star-shaped glint.
- **B, character (hatching):** 0.2 s a zigzag crack opens across the cream top / 1-3 s the cap lifts like a lid, a
  fluffy yellow chick head pushes up, its eye pops on / 4 s its orange beak opens chirping and two stubby wings flap
  up, the shell cap perched on its head.
- **C, celebration (sprout):** 0.2 s a green shoot pokes out of the cream top / 1-3 s it uncurls, two round leaves
  open / 4 s a candy bud on top pops open into a small pink flower.

**Pick: B, hatching.** The cream cap on a pale pill is an eggshell top, so it cracking and a chick popping out is the
shape's own nature (5a rule 1) with a friendly character beat (rule 2), and a chirp suits a high A5: quick, bright,
small (the chick about 150 px). Eye-wakes is runner-up but eyes waking are already common; the sprout's parts are
small and generic.
