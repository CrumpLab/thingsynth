# note-82 A#5 (f078)

**What it is:** a small lime-green hook (about 140x135), curled like a leaf or a comma, tucked in the notch where the foot of the peach-and-blue capsule meets the top of the big yellow pod below it; its inner edge is a single curl line, and the coral antler hand's pale prongs rise to its right.

- **A fiddlehead (own-nature sprout):** 0.2 s its curled tip flicks up; 1-3 s it unrolls into a fern frond, fat lobed leaflets popping in pairs up its length; 4 s a second little fiddlehead pops out beside it.
- **B chameleon (character):** 0.2 s its curl tightens into a coiled tail; 1-3 s a lumpy lime chameleon body and head pop up on the pod, its turret eye swivels; 4 s its tongue zaps out and curls, and it flushes candy stripes.
- **C pea pod (pop):** 0.2 s it splits along its seam; 1-3 s three fat peas pop out one after another, bouncing up; 4 s the last pea pops highest with a glint.

**Pick: B, the chameleon.** The hook already is a chameleon's curled tail, so the shape becomes an animal that could sit on that pod (rule 1), and the tongue zap is a quick, bright, playful action for a high note (rule 2). The fern is good own nature but leans on small leaflets; the peas repeat the popcorn's popping.
