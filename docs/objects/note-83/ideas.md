# note-83 B5 (f293): ideas

**What it is.** The ochre tip of the horned mask's left horn (f293, 128x179), a curved crescent hooking up and left at
the painting's top-left corner, the blue-grey horn (note-44) running down from it.

- **A. Crescent moon (own-nature morph).** 0.2 s: the tip brightens and a first little star pops beside it / 1-3 s: it
  fattens into a lemon-yellow crescent moon with a sleepy profile (a nose bump, a closed lid), a star pops each second
  into the corner / 4 s: the moon smiles and the stars twinkle.
- **B. Tipping its hat (character).** 0.2 s: the tip lifts a crack off the horn / 1-3 s: it rises and tilts sideways in
  greeting, a pink tuft showing underneath / 4 s: held high, motion crescents round it.
- **C. Shooting star (celebration).** 0.2 s: a glint on its point / 1-3 s: it streaks off up and left as a comet, a
  candy tail stretching behind / 4 s: the tail fans out in bands.

**Pick: A, crescent moon.** The crescent already is a moon in shape, up in the sky at the top of the painting, so it
changes in its own nature (rule 1), wakes a little face (rule 2) and brings small bright pops for a high note (stars
the size of the tip, not glitter). The hat is a costume reading, the comet flies off the painting's edge.
