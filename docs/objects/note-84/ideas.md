# note-84 C6 (f120): ideas

**The shape:** the small pale yellow teardrop (#FFE47F, 101x132) in the lower left of the eye tree's half-lidded
almond eye, under its dark olive lid. It is the lit part of the eye whose crown becomes the songbird on Upper 2'
(upper-db6 wakes this eye round on its last step, and keeps f120 in front).

- **A, own-nature morph: a happy tear.** The teardrop is a tear. 0.2 s: it bulges at its tip. 1 s: it slides out of
  the eye. 2-3 s: it rolls fatter down the crown's cheek. 4 s: it splashes into a crown of candy droplets.
- **B, character: a wink.** 0.2 s: a dark pupil pops into the yellow. 1-3 s: the pupil looks left, then up, and the
  dark lid drops shut in a wink. 4 s: the lid springs back up, and a fat lash curl flicks out.
- **C, playful pun: eye candy.** The yellow teardrop becomes a sweet. 0.2 s: a candy stripe and a shine pop across it.
  1-3 s: twisted wrapper ends flick out either side, and it pops out of the eye, growing. 4 s: it hangs in front of
  the eye as a big striped wrapped sweet, about 180 px, with a sparkle.

**Pick: C, eye candy.** It is a playful literal pun drawn big (rule 4), it is quick and bright for a high note, and it
is the shape itself turning into the sweet. A is own-nature but a tear leans sad. B would fight upper-db6, which
already wakes this eye (and eyes waking are common), so C avoids a second eye action on the same eye.
