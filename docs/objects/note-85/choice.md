# note-85 C#6: choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A fiddlehead (the curl unrolls into a fern frond), B shrimp (bead eye, bands, fan tail,
antennae), C party blower (it unrolls straight, candy-striped, with a tassel and a toot).

Picked C, the party blower. The red curl is exactly the rolled-up shape of a party blower, so it unrolls in its own
nature and is a playful literal pun drawn big and bright; celebratory, with a toot for a high note. The fern repeats the
painting's many plants and the shrimp is a small fiddly face.
