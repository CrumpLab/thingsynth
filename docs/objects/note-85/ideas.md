# note-85 C#6 (f153)

**The shape:** a small red hooked curl (138x104) coming off the top right of the blue ring-creature, bottom left of
centre, bending over to the right like a little tail or a rolled-up feeler; the grey lying creature's body is
behind it. No face.

- **A fiddlehead (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s: the hook coils in tighter at its tip; 1-3 s: it unrolls into a
  fern frond, lumpy candy-green leaflets popping out along it in pairs; 4 s: the tip uncurls fully with a last
  pair of leaflets.
- **B shrimp (character):** 0.2 s: a black bead eye pops at the hook's root; 1-3 s: segment bands come up along
  it, a fan tail spreads at its tip and long antennae flick up; 4 s: it flips its tail with motion crescents.
- **C party blower (celebration, pun):** 0.2 s: the curl starts to unroll and a mouthpiece pops on its root;
  1-3 s: it unrolls straight, candy-striped, out to the right; 4 s: a feather tassel pops at its tip and two
  fat toot puffs burst out.

**Pick: C, the party blower.** The red curl is exactly the rolled-up shape of a party blower, so it unrolls in its
own nature (rule 1) and is a playful literal pun drawn big and bright (rule 4), celebratory (rule 3), with a toot
for a high note. The fern repeats the painting's many plants; the shrimp is a small fiddly face.
