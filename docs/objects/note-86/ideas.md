# note-86 D6 (f277)

**What it is:** the pink upper lid (#FF4A66 at 0.51, 153x60) of the sleepy eye on the big grey-green mushroom cap,
top centre: a half-moon over the lilac eyeball, with a lash flicking out right and a curled brow-hook on its left.

- **A, wide awake with lashes (own nature: the lid lifts):** 0.2 s: the lid twitches up a crack, a white sliver
  shows. 1-3 s: it lifts in steps, the white, a cyan iris and pupil appear, its lash flick grows into three long
  curling candy lashes. 4 s: the eye wide open, glint on.
- **B, wink (character):** 0.2 s: the brow-hook pops up. 1-3 s: the lid lifts showing the eye, then drops
  in a squeezed wink, a pink cheek blush swells under it. 4 s: a big fat yellow twinkle star pops at the corner.
- **C, ladybug (morph):** the domed pink lid is a ladybug's shell. 0.2 s: a seam cracks down its middle and black
  spots pop. 1-3 s: the two halves lift like wing cases, filmy wings flick out, its little head and feelers peek
  out the side. 4 s: it buzzes up off the eye and hovers above the cap, leaving the eye open beneath, looking up at
  it.

**Pick: C, the ladybug, ending with the eye open.** The lid becomes something living that could sit on a mushroom
(rule 1), its own dome splitting like the party cone's beak, with the eye waking underneath as the last beat
(rule 2), small, bright and quick for a high note. A is only an eye opening; B's star is a sticker.
