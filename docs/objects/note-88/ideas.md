# note-88 E6 (f033): ideas

**What it is:** the see-through raspberry patch (#FF543C at 0.52 over the wash, 93x88) at the bottom of the purple
trunk creature's oval, striped head (green band on top, cyan in the middle), like its lower lip or cheek; the brown
striped shell is to its left and the pale pink creature behind it.

- **A Raspberry fruit (own nature):** the raspberry-red patch becomes a raspberry. 0.2 s: a first fat drupelet
  bulges out of it; 1-3 s: drupelets pop on round it one after another into a lumpy berry about 150 px across;
  4 s: a green calyx star unfurls on its top and a white shine pops on.
- **B Blowing a raspberry (character / pun):** the patch is its lip. 0.2 s: the lip pouts out; 1-3 s: it opens and a
  fat pink tongue pushes out between the lips, wobbling longer; 4 s: "pfft": three lumpy spit droplets and a puff fly
  off the tongue tip.
- **C Kiss (celebration):** the lip puckers into a big kiss. 0.2 s: the lip swells into a pout; 1-3 s: a red candy
  kiss-pucker forms and a first pink heart pops off it, then a second, bigger; 4 s: a third big heart floats up with a
  white shine.

**Pick: B, blowing a raspberry.** A character doing something playful with its own mouth (5a rule 2), drawn as a big
literal pun on the patch's raspberry colour (rule 4), quick and bright for a high note. The fruit morph has no
character, and the hearts are symbols.
