# note-89 F6 (f294): ideas

**What it is.** The ochre tip of the horned mask's right horn (f294, 123x124), a small hook curling up and left on top
of the dark teal horn, in the open cream sky under the painting's top edge, the coral-orange hood to its right.

- **A. Pincer (own-nature morph).** 0.2 s: the hook splits at its base / 1-3 s: the two halves open into a crab's
  pincer, knobbly teeth along the inside / 4 s: it snaps shut with click crescents.
- **B. Hatching chick (character).** 0.2 s: the tip cracks like an eggshell top / 1 s: a fuzzy yellow head pushes up
  under it / 2 s: a stubby wing flaps out / 3 s: its beak opens / 4 s: it chirps with the shell perched on its head as a
  hat, a little song crescent beside it.
- **C. Sprout and bloom (celebration).** 0.2 s: a green shoot pokes out of the hook / 1-3 s: two leaves unfurl and a bud
  rises / 4 s: a candy flower pops open.

**Pick: B, hatching chick.** The highest note in the batch gets a chirp: a character doing something small, bright and
quick (rule 2), and the hooked tip reads as a broken shell cap, so the shape itself becomes the eggshell rather than a
prop. The pincer was the runner-up (the hook's own form splitting) but crab claws lost before; the sprout repeats the
painting's many plants.
