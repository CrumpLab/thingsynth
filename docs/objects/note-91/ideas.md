# note-91 G6: f307, the little cream nook under the straw

**What it is:** a tiny see-through cream gap (#FFFCCA at 0.68, ~125x80, no ink of its own), a rounded triangle of sky
tucked under the straw's arch, between the green stalk's leaf bud on its right and the cream snout creature's pink
head below.

- **A, dewdrop (own-nature):** 0.2 s a bright drop beads on the straw's underside / 1-3 s it swells and a second
  drop forms beside it / 4 s the first falls with a little splash crown on the snout's head.
- **B, chick (character):** 0.2 s a speckled egg pops into the nook / 1-3 s the egg cracks in a zigzag and a yellow
  chick's head pushes up out of it / 4 s its beak opens wide in a chirp, a little note-burst over it.
- **C, lantern (celebration):** 0.2 s a candy paper lantern pops open hanging from the straw's arch / 1-3 s its
  ribs fill with colour band by band and a tassel drops / 4 s it glows, a bright core lighting up.

**Pick: B, chick.** The nook under the arch is a nest, so a living thing hatches in it (rules 1 and 2), and a chirp is
the brightest, quickest reading of a high note: egg at a tap, crack, head, chirp. A is pale on pale and small, and C
hangs a prop.
