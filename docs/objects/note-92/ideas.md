# note-92 G#6 (f008): ideas

**What it is.** A small solid pink-red (#F64C76) lower lip or tongue, 177x68, in the open mouth of the sleepy green
creature at the bottom left (the rotor creature: round green head, closed eye, cream ear and baguette arm), a white
tooth standing just above its left end, the lavender ground below.

- **A, own-nature morph: blep.** 0.2 s: the tongue tip pokes out past the lip. 1-3 s: it lolls out further, fat and
  lumpy, down and to the left. 4 s: its tip curls up and a drop of drool beads on it.
- **B, character: whistle.** 0.2 s: the lips purse into a round pink O. 1-3 s: little round puffs pop out of it one a
  second, each a different candy colour, drifting up-left. 4 s: a puffed cheek swells beside the mouth and a fat last
  puff blows out.
- **C, playful pun: bubblegum.** 0.2 s: a small pink bubble pops out between the lips. 1-3 s: it swells steadily, a
  white shine on it. 4 s: it is big (about 200 px) and starting to wobble, a second shine and a thin stretched spot.

**Pick: C, bubblegum.** The pink lip literally blows itself bigger, so the hold length is the bubble's size, the most
direct reading of a held note; it is playful and physical (rules 2 and 4), small and bright for a high note, and one
fat shape reads at a glance. The blep repeats the tongues already built (the forked coral tongue, the pink figure's
tongue); the whistle's puffs drift into a busy area.
