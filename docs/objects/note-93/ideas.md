# note-93 A6 (f018): ideas

**What it is.** A tiny see-through pale yellow triangle (#FFF9AD at 56%, 82x109) wedged at the right tip of the
striped stack's pink cap, where the cap meets the big grey lying creature, the narwhal's lavender-teal mitten arm
rising just above it.

- **A, own-nature morph: candy corn.** It is a pale triangle. 0.2 s: an orange band fills its middle. 1-3 s: a yellow
  base band, it plumps up glossy, then a second candy corn tips out beside it. 4 s: a third one lands on top with a
  white shine.
- **B, character: bumblebee.** 0.2 s: a black stripe pops across it. 1-3 s: two more stripes, a round head with
  antennae bumps out on its tip, two see-through wings flick open. 4 s: its eye opens bright and motion crescents
  buzz round it.
- **C, playful pun: the triangle.** The shape is a triangle. 0.2 s: it turns into a silver percussion triangle frame
  with an open corner. 1-3 s: a beater taps it and the first ring crescent goes out, then a second. 4 s: three ring
  crescents and a star-shaped glint at the struck corner.

**Pick: B, the bumblebee.** The pale yellow triangle becomes a small living thing that could be there (rule 1) and
does something quick and bright, its wings flicking open and buzzing (rule 2), right for a high note; the first
stripe is clear at a tap. The triangle pun is a prop, and the candy corns multiplying echo the popcorn.
