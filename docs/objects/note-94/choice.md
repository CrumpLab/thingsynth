# note-94 A#6: choice

Offered (see ideas.md): A wide awake (the lid lifts, a gold iris looks round), B wink (it opens, blushes and winks with
a tongue out), C starstruck (the eye snaps open, its pupil turns into a candy star, small stars pop round it).

Picked C, starstruck, with A's opening as its first beat. The shape is the white of a sleepy eye, so its own nature is
to open; the star pupil and popping stars make it the celebration and give a small, bright, quick payoff for a high
note. The wink is fiddly at this size.
