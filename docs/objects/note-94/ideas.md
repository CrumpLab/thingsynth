# note-94 A#6 (f258)

**The shape:** the pale cream lower half (112x49) of the half-lidded eye of the lime-green blob, left of centre,
just above the pink cross and the gold ring; the lid line runs straight across its top. It is the white of a
sleepy eye.

- **A wide awake (own nature):** 0.2 s: the lid line lifts a crack and more white shows; 1-3 s: the eye opens to
  a full round eyeball, a gold iris and pupil come in and look round; 4 s: it is wide, glossy and staring up,
  with a white glint.
- **B wink (character action):** 0.2 s: the white bulges and the lid starts to lift; 1-3 s: the eye opens and
  looks over at the pink bar beside it, a pink blush comes up under it; 4 s: it winks shut in a cheeky
  curve with a little tongue poking out below.
- **C starstruck (celebration):** 0.2 s: the lid snaps up and the white swells; 1-3 s: a candy pupil comes in
  and turns into a fat five-point star, a second star glint by it; 4 s: three small candy stars pop out round
  the eye.

**Pick: C, starstruck (with A's opening as its first beat).** It is the batch's one eye, and the shape is an eye,
so its own nature is to open (rules 1 and 2); the star pupil and the popping stars make it the celebration
(rule 3) and the small, bright, quick payoff a high note wants. The wink is fun but fiddly at this size.
