# note-95 B6 (f154)

**The shape:** a thin red-orange sliver (209x84), rounded at its left end, poking out left from behind the upper
side of the big gold ring, bottom left, near the painting's left edge; a matching red sliver curls out of the ring's
underside. It reads as a lick of flame, a tongue or a little tail.

- **A flame (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s: the sliver's tip flicks up; 1-3 s: it flares into a lick of fire, a
  second and third tongue of flame curling up off it, with a yellow core; 4 s: sparks pop off the top.
- **B mouse (character, pun on the squeak):** 0.2 s: two round pink ears pop up on its rounded left end;
  1-3 s: the end swells into a little red mouse head, a pink nose and whiskers come out, then an eye opens;
  4 s: its mouth opens in a squeak and a thin tail flicks up behind.
- **C hot chili (pun):** 0.2 s: the sliver plumps up glossy; 1-3 s: it swells into a fat red chili with a green
  cap and stalk; 4 s: steam puffs and a little jet of flame burst out of its tip.

**Pick: B, the mouse.** The sliver itself becomes a creature that could live there (rule 1) and does something
(rule 2), and the squeak is a playful reading of the second-highest note (rule 4), all small, bright and quick.
The flame is close to the rocket's and pyramid's flames; the chili is a pun with no character.
