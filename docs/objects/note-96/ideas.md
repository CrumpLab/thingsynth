# note-96 C7 (f200)

**What it is:** the single egg-shaped olive-yellow (#DBD27D) eye, 59 x 90, on the straw creature's tan head at the
top right, between its tan ears, above its pink lip and orange collar (the head its upper drawbar rings with candy
petals).

- **A. Gold star (own-nature morph):** 0.2 s a first point juts out of the oval / 1-3 s the other points push out one
  by one into a fat lumpy gold star / 4 s two little candy stars pop either side, twinkling.
- **B. Wink (character action):** 0.2 s a dark pupil pops in / 1-3 s it looks up at the petals, then a heavy tan lid
  swings down / 4 s a full wink with three thick lashes and a candy sparkle at its corner.
- **C. Heart eyes (celebration):** 0.2 s the top of the oval dips into a heart's notch / 1-3 s it swells into a big
  glossy pink heart that beats bigger / 4 s two small hearts float up off it.

**Pick: C, heart eyes.** Rules 1-3: the eye itself morphs, it gives the one-eyed creature a smitten expression, and
it is bright and quick for a high note; the heart gets about 150 px across. The wink keeps the eye as it is; the star
is a morph without feeling.
