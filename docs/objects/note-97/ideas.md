# note-97 C#7 (f046): ideas

**What it is:** a little salmon-pink nub (#F7A19E, 45x102) poking straight up from behind the top of the pale pink
creature's round back, like a stubby antenna or a worm peeking out, under the white froth and the brown trunk.

- **A Bud (own nature):** the nub is a bud. 0.2 s: its tip splits and a first pink petal peeks out; 1-3 s: a short
  green stem lifts it, two leaves unfurl, the petals open one by one; 4 s: a candy sweet-pea blossom about 150 px across
  with a yellow middle.
- **B Peeking worm (character):** the nub is a pink worm coming out of a hole in the creature's back. 0.2 s: its tip
  bobs up and a round eye pops open; 1-3 s: it stretches up in lumpy rings, leaning left and then right to look round;
  4 s: its mouth opens in a chirp and a little yellow tuft springs up on its head.
- **C Birthday candle (celebration / pun):** the nub on the round pink back is a candle on a cake. 0.2 s: a flame
  flicks on at its tip; 1-3 s: candy stripes wind up it, the flame grows tall and a wax drip runs down; 4 s: a ring of
  fat candy sparks pops round the flame.

**Pick: B, peeking worm.** The nub's own form comes alive as a creature that could live there (5a rules 1 and 2), and a
quick, bobbing chirp suits a high note. The bud is plain growth without character; the candle was already passed over
as a prop on the horned mask.
