# note-98 D7 (f041): ideas

**What it is:** a thin pale ice-blue sliver (#D2F0F5, about 25 px wide, 94x196) slanting up-right across the pale pink
creature's legs, like a quill, an icicle or a straw, with a matching pink sliver to its left.

- **A Feather (own nature):** the sliver is a quill. 0.2 s: the first barbs fringe out at its tip; 1-3 s: pale blue
  vanes unfurl down both sides, with lilac and pink bands and a few ragged splits; 4 s: the tip curls over and a fluffy
  down tuft puffs out at its base.
- **B Dragonfly (character):** the sliver is a dragonfly's body. 0.2 s: two round cyan eyes pop on its top end;
  1-3 s: a first pair of see-through wings flicks open, then the second pair, and its body gets candy bands; 4 s: it
  lifts off its spot (a raised copy with motion crescents behind), wings wide.
- **C Sparkler (celebration / pun):** the thin stick is a sparkler. 0.2 s: its tip lights in a yellow spark; 1-3 s:
  fat candy sparks spray out in a widening burst, the stick glowing down from the tip; 4 s: a full lumpy starburst
  about 180 px across.

**Pick: B, dragonfly.** The sliver itself becomes a creature that could live there (5a rule 1), with a face and wings
that do something (rule 2), quick and bright for a high note. The feather is a quiet morph; the sparkler a fine
celebration but close to plain sparkles.
