# note-99 D#7 (f246): ideas

**What it is:** the olive lower half (salmon at 0.46 over the lime wash, 87x67) of the little green bean in the party
narwhal's mouth, under its slit eye, cut across by its jaw line, bottom left.

- **A Sprouting bean (own nature):** the bean sprouts. 0.2 s: a pale root tip pokes out of its bottom; 1-3 s: a green
  shoot loops up out of it and straightens, two seed leaves open; 4 s: a curly tendril and a third leaf.
- **B Bubblegum (character):** the narwhal is chewing gum. 0.2 s: a small pink bubble pops out of its mouth; 1-3 s: it
  swells bigger and rounder, a lighter shine and a fold at its neck coming in; 4 s: a big lumpy pink bubble about 200
  px across, its shine wobbling, just about to pop.
- **C Party blower (celebration):** the bean is the mouthpiece of a party blower. 0.2 s: a striped tube pokes out;
  1-3 s: it unrolls out of the mouth in candy stripes, curl by curl; 4 s: it is fully out, its paper tassel flaring.

**Pick: B, bubblegum.** The character does something playful with its own mouth (5a rule 2) and the bean's own form
swells (rule 1); a bubble growing to bursting point is the brightest, poppiest high-note shape. The sprout is plant
growth the painting already has a lot of; the blower is a prop.
