# voice-split: the purple ledge

## Log entry (section 5)

- **Purple ledge review, split point (2026-10-28, object picked by Claude):** the ledge (f165) is a long shelf of land
  right of the stream, #C372D7 at 0.7 over the lime ground so it reads dusty mauve. It has a level top edge and a
  torn, frilly hem hanging over the lime frill below. The pink tongue crosses its upper left, the blue fish's fin
  covers its right end, and the orange capsule and the coral antler hand stand in front of its hem. It has no face or
  limbs. The parameter is display only, C4 to C5 in 12 semitone steps: the key where one keyboard divides into lower
  and upper manuals. Offered giant clam (an upper shell lifts open, frilly mantles, a pearl), axolotl (its left end
  rears into a smiling head with candy gills), split keyboard (an octave of big candy keys rises from its top),
  banana split (scoops, cream and cherries on a banana along its top) and grapes (bunches hang from its hem under vine
  leaves). Picked the giant clam on its own (under 5a). The ledge turns into something that could live by a stream,
  and its frilly hem is already a clam's mantle (rule 1). It also reads the parameter literally as a big drawn thing:
  one shell splits into an upper and a lower half, the way the split point divides one keyboard into two manuals
  (rule 4). The pearl gives it a celebratory last beat. The axolotl was the runner-up as the character option. The
  banana split was a pun on a prop, the keyboard literal but mechanical, and the grapes decoration.
  Adjusted on pick: the first lid read as a fan of straight rays, which echoed the big eye's peacock fan. It was
  redrawn as a lumpy clam shell with a scalloped rim and a few broad, wavy pale flutes. The mantles were redrawn as
  lumpy, hand-drawn ruffles.
  Built as one arrival per semitone, each landing on k/12. Over steps 1-6 the upper shell rises from behind the
  fish's fin and lifts open, one pose per step (2 to 24 degrees), with each pose swinging in quickly at the end of its
  step. The dark plum mouth and a thin pink lip show under the shell as it opens. On step 7 the lower mantle frills out
  along the ledge's top as a mint ruffle, left to right. On step 8 the upper mantle frills out under the shell's lip as
  a pink ruffle, from the hinge outward. Each ruffle is built from overlapping pieces, with their middles laid again
  fill-only so they join without seams. Over steps 9-11 a pearl swells in the mouth, one size a step. On step 12 it
  shines: a white highlight and a glint of cyan at its rim. The effect is placed `around` with an empty role (`open`),
  so the ledge is not masked and the shell sits over its top edge. The fish (f179) and the capsule (f169) stay in
  front.
